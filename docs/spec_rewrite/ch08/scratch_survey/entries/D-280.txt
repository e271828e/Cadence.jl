11566: ### D-280 — Build a `Dual` activation of every component in CI
11567: 
11568: **Status.** ratified
11569: 
11570: **Position.** The repository's test suite builds a `Dual` activation of every
11571: component, with `build(world; activations = (Float64, ProbeDual))`.
11572: Linearizability is an invariant held by this policy.
11573: 
11574: **Spec.** [§8.2][s8-2], [§9.4][s9-4], [Appendix B][sB]
11575: 
11576: **Rationale.** Activations are lazy ([D-052][d-052]), so a successful `build` does not
11577: certify the model linearizable. A pinned `Float64` hidden in a constructor, or
11578: a misplaced `Pinned` leaf, lurks until the first `Dual` activation. The policy
11579: moves that first activation into CI. An activation is a re-run of the
11580: activation step alone, cheap per the kernel prototype ([D-166][d-166]). [D-166][d-166]'s annotation records
11581: that the policy survived [D-166][d-166]'s supersession by [D-263][d-263]. [D-099][d-099] spells the probe
11582: scalar, and [D-275][d-275] makes the keyword the whole entry point.
11583: 
11584: **Rejected.**
11585: - *Eager `Dual` at every build:* doubles compile latency for a CI-only
11586:   guarantee. (As recorded in [D-052][d-052].)
