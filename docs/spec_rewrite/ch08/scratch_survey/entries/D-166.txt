5718: ### D-166 — Mandate `Type{T}` output signatures on continuous producers
5719: 
5720: **Status.** superseded → [D-263][d-263]
5721: 
5722: **Position.** `output_types(::C, ::Type{T}) where {T <: Real}` is **mandated on
5723: continuous producers**; plain `output_types(::C)` is **mandated on discrete
5724: producers**; both are Stratum-A-checkable (tier is known from declaration
5725: shape, [§8.5][s8-5]), so the whole-signature forgotten-`T` variant — the nastiest
5726: member of the class in the [D-033][d-033] world, where the plain form *was* the tier
5727: marker — is extinct by construction.
5728: 
5729: **Spec.** [§7.1][s7-1], [§7.3][s7-3], [§8.1][s8-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.5][s8-5], [§9.4][s9-4], [§9.5][s9-5], [§14.10][s14-10], [Appendix D][sD]
5730: 
5731: **Rationale.** Semantics are **literal**: cell types per activation = the
5732: declaration evaluated at that activation's `T`; the output-side leaf walk is
5733: deleted.
5734: 
5735: Per leaf: `T` (alone or as a parameter, `MyStruct{T}`) = participating;
5736: `Float64` = deliberately pinned — **schema-visible whole-leaf freezing,
5737: delivering [§14.10][s14-10]'s recorded door** (declare `Float64`, strip with `value()` in
5738: the stage; the freeze is declared and conformance-checked); `Int`/`Bool`/ enum
5739: and reference-typed leaves as before.
5740: 
5741: [§9.5][s9-5]'s embed-accept is re-keyed on declared-`T` leaves: a `T` leaf accepts
5742: exactly {activation scalar, `Float64`-embedded-as-zero-partial}; the
5743: constant-branch idiom (`flow > 0 ? f(x) : 0.0`) stays legal as written.
5744: 
5745: An **observed `Dual` at a declared-pinned leaf** has one honest cause and gets
5746: the didactic hint: "if `F` participates in differentiation, declare it `T`".
5747: 
5748: The residual per-leaf forgotten-`T` is a lurking-loud class (caught at first
5749: `Dual`-activation build, never silent — no lossy `Dual → Float64` cast exists),
5750: contained by an **adopted CI policy: the test suite builds a `Dual` activation
5751: of every component** (activations are Stratum-C re-runs; cheap, per the kernel
5752: prototype).
5753: 
5754: Macro sugar over the declaration layer is a recorded door, compatible with
5755: [§8.1][s8-1]'s position (optional, on top, never required).
5756: 
5757: `init_x`/`init_m`/`init_z` **stay one-argument**, and the criterion is
5758: recorded: **by-value declarations state nominal physics** (their *types* walk
5759: by rule — [§7.1][s7-1] forces every state leaf to follow the activation scalar;
5760: partials enter by per-invocation seeding, never initialization); **by-type
5761: declarations are functions of the activation scalar**; **by-allocation takes
5762: the scalar** ([D-077][d-077]'s `workspace(c, T)` precedent).
5763: 
5764: Reversal grounds vs [D-079][d-079], recorded so it is never re-litigated from a worse
5765: memory: reader-honesty revalued (the sole author is also the six-months-later
5766: reader; the plain nominal declaration is misleading to anyone not carrying the
5767: walk rule in their head), and two of the three grounds that decided [D-079][d-079] had
5768: already dissolved independently — tier-by-declaration-shape removed the
5769: tier-marker trap, and embed-accept removed the constant-branch detonation that
5770: made the original `T`-form look fragile. [D-079][d-079]'s two conceded losses
5771: ("schema-visible participation, whole-leaf stripping detection — tooling
5772: niceties") are re-valued as the point.
5773: 
5774: Annotation (post-[D-173][d-173] re-examination, 2026-08-16): with `init_z` fused into
5775: `init_x`, the discrete tier also declares `init_x`, and the one-argument ruling
5776: was re-examined and holds — the discrete form stays plain by the same tier rule
5777: as `output_types`, and [§7.1][s7-1]'s ground is fusion-independent. The criterion's
5778: sharpest form, recorded so the next re-examination starts here: a `T` belongs
5779: in a signature exactly where the declaration's non-nominal behavior is
5780: underdetermined by its nominal restriction — by the per-leaf pin/walk choice on
5781: output contracts (this entry), by the tolerant/demands-frozen choice on input
5782: contracts ([D-167][d-167]), by vocabulary freedom on workspace ([D-077][d-077], which no walk
5783: rule could cover) — and nowhere else: [§7.1][s7-1]'s walk being total, a two-argument
5784: `init_x`'s output at any `T` is fully recoverable from its one-argument
5785: restriction, so its `T` would carry no information, only ceremony on values and
5786: a new forgotten-`T` class caught only at activation build.
5787: 
5788: Annotation (2026-09-08): the per-leaf statement of embed-accept is decided on
5789: the type, by lifting and exact comparison, per [D-238][d-238].
5790: 
5791: Annotation (2026-09-24): superseded by [D-263][d-263]. The literal semantics and the
5792: two-argument form go; the walk returns on the output side with a leaf-level
5793: `Pinned{P}` marker, the element this entry's reversal of [D-079][d-079] lacked. The
5794: embed-accept relation, the CI policy and the criterion recorded in the
5795: 2026-08-16 annotation all survive; the criterion is what the marker
5796: satisfies at the leaf instead of the signature.
5797: 
5798: **Rejected.**
5799: - *Plain declaration + leaf walk on the output side ([D-079][d-079]'s design):*
5800:   writer-honest — nothing to forget, no lurk — but reader-opaque, and
5801:   deliberate pinning of a real leaf inexpressible.
5802: - *The one-argument `Float64` fallback on continuous producers:* reopens the
5803:   whole-signature hole just closed; the "doesn't care about AD" population is ≈
5804:   empty in a flight library — trim and linearization sweep whole aircraft
5805:   assemblies — so the fallback mostly manufactures components that poison
5806:   model-wide `Dual` activations at someone else's first trim.
5807: - *Two-argument `init_x` by evaluation:* the `T` records no choice — [§7.1][s7-1]
5808:   admits no pinned state leaf; `zero(T)`/constructor ceremony on values; a new
5809:   forgotten-`T` class *on values* caught only at activation build; the didactic
5810:   side-by-side reading is bought instead by the recorded signature criterion.
5811: - *Two-argument `init_m`/`init_z`:* nothing could ever follow `T`; the
5812:   signature would invite exactly the misplacement [§8.2][s8-2]'s didactic errors exist
5813:   to catch.
5814: - *Blanket `T`-signatures on all declarations:* ritual dilutes the signal — the
5815:   reader-honesty of `T` depends on its appearing exactly where a choice was
5816:   made; the criterion, not uniformity, is the rule.
