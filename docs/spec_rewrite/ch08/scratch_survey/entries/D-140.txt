4523: ### D-140 — Add a three-rung remedy ladder for artificial algebraic-loop dependencies
4524: 
4525: **Status.** ratified
4526: 
4527: **Position.** [§5.4][s5-4]'s escape hatch becomes a **three-rung remedy ladder** for
4528: artificial loops; [§8.3][s8-3] and [§4.3][s4-3] are unchanged.
4529: 
4530: **Spec.** [§4.3][s4-3], [§5.4][s5-4], [§5.6][s5-6], [§8.3][s8-3]
4531: 
4532: **Rationale.** (i) The **two-stage split** dissolves most of the class —
4533: the `VehicleDynamics` instance of `flight_case_studies.md` is the canonical dissolution. (ii)
4534: **Contract re-factoring**, the new middle rung: when [§5.6][s5-6]'s tracer says
4535: *artificial*, re-examine the cycle's wires before moving any code, because an
4536: input the neighbor consumes **only in a fallback branch** is the archetypal
4537: false dependency — the neighbor is computing, on the component's behalf, a
4538: fallback whose semantics belong on the component's own side of the boundary;
4539: move the branch to its natural owner and the wire disappears. Canonical
4540: instance, taken from the shipped code the finding walked
4541: (`landinggear.jl:255-323`, `:63-72`): `Strut` hands the contact-velocity
4542: azimuth `ψ_v` to the steering model and consumes the returned `ψ_sw` throughout
4543: the contact-frame construction, but `ψ_v` is used *only* in the steering's
4544: disengaged (castoring) branch, and castoring is free-swiveling wheel physics —
4545: the strut's business, not the steering law's; re-factoring `AbstractSteering`
4546: to emit `(engaged, ψ_cmd)` and computing `ψ_sw = engaged ? ψ_cmd : ψ_v` inside
4547: `Strut` deletes the backward wire, and the factoring **survives substitution**
4548: — a stateful steering actuator produces `ψ_cmd` from its own state and still
4549: needs nothing from the strut — which is the test that it records structure
4550: rather than dodging the diagnostic. (iii) **Splitting the component** stays the
4551: residual remedy, for cycles whose halves genuinely belong to one component, and
4552: its cost is now **stated where it bites**: [§8.3][s8-3]'s visibility is binary, so
4553: every intermediate shared across the new boundary becomes `output_types` —
4554: public, connectable, substitution-relevant. The cap is [§4.3][s4-3]'s granularity
4555: guideline, which the split case satisfies trivially (one producing stage, one
4556: consumer): **one struct-valued bundle port** (a `StrutGeometry`-shaped value),
4557: not N loose ports — the bundle type becomes contract, a real cost but a bounded
4558: and honest one. [§5.6][s5-6]'s hint offers **both exits** ("split this component, or
4559: narrow the neighbor's contract"), continuous members only as before, and the
4560: classifier machinery is untouched: it already knows which hop died. [§5.4][s5-4]'s
4561: "rare" is thereby **earned** rather than asserted — the split is rare *because*
4562: rungs (i) and (ii) absorb the common shapes. `migration_outline.md` records the strut/steering
4563: pair as the contract-re-factoring worked instance, beside the dissolving
4564: `VehicleDynamics` of `flight_case_studies.md`, with the split and its bundle port noted as the residual
4565: remedy not taken; the `AbstractSteering` contract change is an
4566: **aircraft-library migration call living in `migration_outline.md`**, not framework vocabulary.
4567: 
4568: **Rejected.**
4569: - *A visibility level for split-orphaned intermediates (`unlisted`,
4570:   `Private(T)`):* an explicit non-reopening of [D-034][d-034] and [D-055][d-055] — the promotion
4571:   cost is real but bounded, and the bundle port keeps it to one name, whereas a
4572:   hiding annotation would buy back that name at the price of "public always
4573:   means someone wrote it down".
4574: - *Leaving [§5.4][s5-4] split-only:* the first real migration instance shows the
4575:   diagnostic steering the author toward mid-computation surgery on seven shared
4576:   intermediates (`l`, `r_bs_b`, `q_bs`, `ks_e`, `v_ec_b_body`, `ξ`, terrain
4577:   data) when deleting one false wire was the answer — and the "rare" claim is
4578:   only true with the ladder in place.
4579: - *Framework-side auto-splitting or auto-narrowing:* the classifier's trace
4580:   speaks only for the branch taken at the probe point ([D-012][d-012]'s diagnostic-only
4581:   doctrine), so remedy selection is the author's — and both [§5.4][s5-4] remedies
4582:   document real structure precisely because a human chose them.
