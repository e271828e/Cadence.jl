10322: ### D-264 — Admit a frozen opaque leaf at a tolerant entry
10323: 
10324: **Status.** ratified
10325: 
10326: **Position.** The wire relation admits a pinned or discrete producer of an
10327: opaque leaf at an unpinned entry of that leaf's type, as the producer's cell.
10328: The identity rule for an opaque leaf ([D-237][d-237]) governs a store and the
10329: producer's own conformance check alone, never a wire.
10330: 
10331: **Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§9.5][s9-5], [Appendix C][sC]
10332: 
10333: **Rationale.** The refusal came from one predicate serving two questions.
10334: `_accepts` decides both the store's conformance check and the wire relation,
10335: and at a store its opaque short-circuit is essential: a producer's output
10336: cell at a `Dual` activation is a `Vector{Handle{Dual}}` when the declaration
10337: walks, and a `Handle{Float64}` cannot be placed there without the framework
10338: constructing a handle, which it refuses to do. At a wire nothing is
10339: constructed. The entry is a bound, and the consumer gathers the producer's
10340: cell as stored. Embedding at a tolerant scalar entry is a type-level
10341: admission plus Julia's promotion inside the consumer's math, and a handle
10342: behaves identically: the consumer reads `u.terrain.h0::Float64` beside `Dual`
10343: state and its arithmetic promotes. The derivative through the handle is zero
10344: along every seeded direction, and that is the true derivative, because the
10345: producer declared that no partials enter it. [§9.5][s9-5]'s argument for why a
10346: `Float64` at a walking leaf is honest applies unchanged. [D-238][d-238]'s parameter
10347: walk already gives the right answer once the short-circuit is lifted: lifting
10348: `Handle{Float64}` at the parameter position where the entry has `T` yields
10349: `Handle{Dual}`, the entry itself.
10350: 
10351: The positive ground is [§8.2][s8-2]'s substitution promise, that a walking producer,
10352: a frozen discrete producer and a root input are all admissible behind a
10353: tolerant entry. For handles that promise was broken in both frozen forms. A
10354: library's landing gear declaring `terrain = TerrainField{Float64}` could not
10355: be fed by a static terrain source at all, and its author could not repair it
10356: without pinning the entry, which would then refuse a moving deck instead.
10357: The wire pass reported the direction under `WalkingFaceAtFrozenEntry` with
10358: the endpoints reversed, calling the entry frozen and the producer walking.
10359: 
10360: Two risks are specific to handles, and both fail loudly at the `Dual` probe.
10361: For numbers Julia promotes a `Float64` and a `Dual` in any expression; for a
10362: handle there is no such machinery, so a consumer signature that ties the
10363: handle's parameter to another argument's (`height(f::H{T}, p::SVector{3,T})
10364: where {T}`) fails with a `MethodError` whenever the handle is frozen and the
10365: position is not. That is the genericity obligation a tolerant entry already
10366: claims, checked by the probe as [§8.2][s8-2] says. And pass-through is refused where
10367: scalar pass-through is embedded: a consumer that re-emits a frozen handle
10368: under an unpinned output hits the store's identity rule, so a handle's pin is
10369: a property of its chain, and every component that forwards it pins that
10370: output too. No silent wrong Jacobian was found. The misplaced-pin class is
10371: covered identically: a pinned producer that builds `Handle(x.h + 1.0, z)` at
10372: a `Dual` activation produces `Handle{Dual}`, the identity check refuses it,
10373: and the pin hint fires. `companions/handle_walk_walkthrough.md` works the
10374: example through.
10375: 
10376: **Rejected.**
10377: - *A named, collected refusal for the direction (a frozen face at a walking
10378:   entry), with remedies to pin the entry or build the handle at `T`:* breaks
10379:   the substitution promise for every frozen handle producer, discrete ones
10380:   included, and asks a library consumer to choose between pinning its entry
10381:   and refusing every static source; the refusal was the store's identity rule
10382:   applied to a question it was not written for.
10383: - *An embedding for opaque leaves (constructing the handle at the entry):*
10384:   the framework never builds handles; a cross-eltype construction can be a
10385:   grid copy per evaluation, which [D-237][d-237]'s identity rule exists to refuse.
