1204: ### D-040 — Slash-string paths as the canonical path form
1205: 
1206: **Status.** ratified
1207: 
1208: **Position.** Slash-string paths, relative to the declaring assembly, one
1209: canonical form shared by declarations, diagnostics, devices and logs.
1210: 
1211: **Spec.** [§8.6][s8-6]
1212: 
1213: **Rationale.** Recorded only through the rejections below.
1214: 
1215: **Rejected.**
1216: - *Instance navigation:* `===`-identical symmetric siblings make
1217:   path-from-instance unrecoverable — proxies remain sugar.
1218: - *Symbol tuples:* structure without readability.
1219: - *Dotted paths:* false Julia-property affordance — the last segment is a port,
1220:   not a field.
