9560: ### D-254 — `Deployment` and `Schedule` as artifacts between the build and the simulation
9561: 
9562: **Status.** ratified
9563: 
9564: **Position.** Deployment binding produces an artifact, and the `Simulation`
9565: materializes it.
9566: 
9567: - `Deployment` is the build plus `h`, `N_base`, `Δt_base`, the algorithm
9568:   and the three event parameters. It is scalar-free. It carries the
9569:   `Schedule`, the grid diagnostics of [D-187][d-187] and its warnings.
9570:   `Simulation` materializes a deployment at `T`. Replay compares two
9571:   deployments as values.
9572: - `Schedule` is the typed schedule inside the deployment: per discrete
9573:   component `(D, Φ, Δt)` with anchor and rate-chain columns, the rate-scope
9574:   rows, and the `D`, `Φ`, `Δt` vectors.
9575: - The grid diagnostics land on the deployment: leave-one-out factors, prime
9576:   attribution, nearest non-refining offsets, the derivation line and
9577:   `GridUtilization`, the last as a deployment warning under [D-250][d-250].
9578: - `Simulation(build; kw...)` and `Simulation(root; kw...)` stay as sugar
9579:   composing the deployment constructor.
9580: 
9581: **Spec.** [§9.2][s9-2], [§10.5][s10-5], [§11.5][s11-5], [§12.7][s12-7], [Appendix C][sC]
9582: 
9583: **Rationale.** Deployment consumes the grid parameters and fixes everything
9584: they determine, the bound schedule and the grid diagnostics among them, and
9585: none of it depends on the scalar type. Today that step ran inside the
9586: `Simulation` constructor and its products lived as `Simulation` fields:
9587: `sched`, `D`, `Φ`, `Δt`, the grid parameters, the event parameters. The
9588: bound schedule was "a named printable artifact on the `Simulation`"
9589: ([D-187][d-187]) with no type of its own, and the grid diagnostics it was to
9590: substrate were never built. A deployment that is an artifact can be
9591: compared, which is what replay's header check does with the deployment
9592: block today by hand, and it can be printed, which is what the anchor and
9593: component tables and the hyperperiod chart need ([D-257][d-257]). It also separates
9594: the two reasons a `Simulation` exists: one build backs many deployments,
9595: one deployment backs many activations at different `T`. The convenience
9596: constructors stay because every existing call site deploys and materializes
9597: in one call, and nothing in the artifact is lost by composing.
9598: 
9599: **Rejected.**
9600: - *Binding inside the `Simulation` constructor, as today:* the products
9601:   have no type, cannot be compared or printed on their own, and the
9602:   `Simulation` carries twelve fields that are not its.
9603: - *The schedule on the `Build`:* it does not exist until `Δt_base` binds,
9604:   and one build backs deployments with different `Δt_base`.
9605: - *A `Deployment{T}`:* nothing in it depends on the scalar; parametrizing
9606:   it would make two deployments at different `T` unequal.
9607: - *Superseded position — the bound schedule as plain data on the
9608:   `Simulation` ([D-187][d-187]):* the artifact it names now has a type and an owner.
