8916: ### D-242 — Name the resolution cases wiring, service and inspection, and retire "register" and "load-bearing" as spec vocabulary
8917: 
8918: **Status.** ratified
8919: 
8920: **Position.** [§13.3][s13-3]'s three resolution cases are named by their client, and
8921: two words leave the design documents' vocabulary, the log included. Every
8922: position stands as ratified; only the words for unchanged concepts move, under
8923: rule 2's word-substitution exception and rule 7's audit. This overturns
8924: [D-122][d-122]'s reservation of "register" for the mode sense.
8925: - *Wiring* replaces [D-130][d-130]'s *structural*: connection declarations, resolved
8926:   in Stratum A under [§6.1][s6-1]'s one-level rule.
8927: - *Service* replaces *load-bearing*: condition entries, trim `reads` and
8928:   taps, checked at the authoring or mount level. "Service" keeps its [§14][s14]
8929:   meaning; the paths are the ones a stopped-sim service compiles.
8930: - *Inspection* replaces *diagnostic*: device read bindings, GUI panels,
8931:   snapshot and log inspection, resolved by the instance walk. It is [D-083][d-083]'s
8932:   own word for that side, so the two vocabularies for one line collapse into
8933:   one. The paired side is *integration reads*.
8934: - "Register" in the sense "a mode or idiom in which something is done" (the
8935:   retired [§D.10][sD-10] entry) is gone. Its compounds are renamed by family: the
8936:   *harness writer* and *what-if replay* (glossary anchors renamed), the run
8937:   *modes* (unattended, test-harness, REPL), usage *idioms* (`step!`,
8938:   test-rig, parallel sweep), the *didactic style* of errors, the
8939:   *by-allocation convention*, the sample time's two *forms*, the naming
8940:   convention's four *classes*, and [§14.4][s14-4]'s *two ways of applying one plan*
8941:   named as the specialized `apply!` and the dynamic walk. The word survives
8942:   only in its storage sense: a slot holding a value.
8943: - "Load-bearing" is dropped outright, its plain-intensifier uses replaced by
8944:   ordinary words.
8945: 
8946: **Spec.** [§8.1][s8-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§10.5][s10-5], [§11.2][s11-2], [§11.3][s11-3], [§11.5][s11-5], [§12.6][s12-6], [§12.7][s12-7], [§13.2][s13-2], [§13.3][s13-3], [§13.5][s13-5],
8947: [§14.4][s14-4], [§14.8][s14-8], [§3.4][s3-4], [§8.6][s8-6], [Appendix A][sA], [§D.10][sD-10]
8948: 
8949: **Rationale.** Four terms failed the cold reader at once. "Register" asked
8950: for a linguistics sense no technical reader reaches for; a heading such as
8951: "the duty is register-scoped" was decodable only from the table beneath it,
8952: and the same word also named storage slots elsewhere in the spec, so a reader
8953: could not tell which sense applied. "Load-bearing" named the right case at a
8954: distance but three quarters of its occurrences were plain intensifier, so the
8955: term of art had no room to be one; it also named the stakes axis where the
8956: spec's own argument turns on where a violation's cost lands. "Diagnostic"
8957: already meant warnings and errors throughout, with an appendix named for
8958: them, and sat a few characters from "didactic". "Structural" was
8959: serviceable but said nothing about wiring. Naming each case by its client
8960: lets the table's row name carry who resolves and, through the chapter the
8961: client belongs to, what is at stake.
8962: 
8963: **Rejected.**
8964: - *"Task", "analysis" or "operation" in place of "service":* "task" collides
8965:   with Julia's `Task`; "analysis" excludes initialization and `capture`;
8966:   "operation" is accurate but buys little over the compounded "stopped-sim
8967:   service" and costs a chapter-wide rename.
8968: - *Naming the middle case by its stakes ("portable" or "relocatable"
8969:   paths):* self-explaining, but the other two rows name clients, and a mixed
8970:   scheme is harder to hold.
8971: - *Keeping "register" in the mode sense with an inline gloss at each first
8972:   use:* the gloss fights the reader's prior at every occurrence instead of
8973:   once.
8974: - *Leaving the log in its old vocabulary:* rule 2's first reading; rejected
8975:   because the substitution moves no position and the old words would mislead
8976:   every later reader of the log, so the exception was written into rule 2.
