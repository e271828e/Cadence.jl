9149: ### D-247 — Accept the `NamedTuple` as the only store declaration form
9150: 
9151: **Status.** ratified
9152: 
9153: **Position.** Every by-value store declaration, `init_x`, `init_s` and
9154: `init_m`, returns a `NamedTuple` with one named field per leaf, and no other
9155: form is admitted. Stratum A checks the form on every primitive before the
9156: tier classifier and the vocabulary checks read the value, and reports a bare
9157: value as `StoreNotNamedTuple`, collected, carrying the store, the observed
9158: type and the wrap that fixes it.
9159: 
9160: **Spec.** [§7.1][s7-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§9.1][s9-1], [Appendix C][sC]
9161: 
9162: **Rationale.** Nothing checked the form, and every reader of a store value
9163: assumes a `NamedTuple`: the tier classifier, the two Stratum A field checks,
9164: `Decls`, the leaf walk and the bundle. Probed on Julia 1.13.0 with leaves
9165: sound otherwise, a `Float64` or an `SVector` from `init_x` and an `Int` from
9166: `init_s` failed as a raw `MethodError` converting to `NamedTuple` in Stratum
9167: C; a `Tuple` from `init_x` failed inside the generated `reconstruct` and one
9168: from `init_s` as a `BoundsError`; a `Vector` from `init_x` segfaulted in the
9169: generated `reconstruct`; a `Symbol` from `init_m` failed as
9170: `isempty(::Symbol)` in the classifier; and an `Int` from `init_m` built
9171: silently as the mode store. The field checks read `pairs(1.0)` without
9172: complaint, so the first refusal came from a stratum that runs user code, or
9173: from the compiler.
9174: 
9175: The bare leaf was weighed as an ergonomic form for one-state components and
9176: rejected on where the field name does work. Every service reaches a leaf by
9177: its field name: the condition overlay is a `NamedTuple` merge keyed on it
9178: ([§14.1][s14-1]), readers and the trace header spell `plant/x.q` ([§11.5][s11-5], [§14.4][s14-4]), the
9179: trim problem's decisions and residuals name it ([§14.7][s14-7]), auto-publication
9180: publishes a state field as the port of the same name ([§5.3][s5-3]), the return laws
9181: pair by it ([§9.5][s9-5]), and the payloads of `IllegalStateLeaf` and
9182: `ConformanceFailure` carry it. A bare leaf would be a second store shape at
9183: each of those seams and would forfeit auto-publication, having no name to
9184: match. The name a one-state component's author is asked for is the name every
9185: service then uses, the discipline `output_types` already asks of ports. The
9186: leaf walk is not the obstacle: an `SArray` lays out wherever it sits.
9187: 
9188: The check collects under [§13.1][s13-1]'s rule for declarative checks and runs first
9189: in the walk's primitive branch, since the classifier and the field checks
9190: cannot read the value. A primitive that fails it records `nothing` for its
9191: tier and is read no further in Stratum A. An empty `(;)` is the fallback's
9192: own value and keeps reading as "declares nothing".
9193: 
9194: Annotation (2026-09-25): the empty store `(;)` is a `NamedTuple` and admitted; it
9195: is the stateless leaf's mandatory tier marker ([D-263][d-263]).
9196: 
9197: **Rejected.**
9198: - *A single leaf as a second store form, addressed as the store itself:* a
9199:   second shape at every name-keyed seam (conditions, readers, trace, trim,
9200:   auto-publication, the return laws, the payloads), no auto-publication for
9201:   it, and no grounding model that wants it, the case studies carrying several
9202:   leaves per component.
9203: - *Canonicalizing a bare value into a one-field `NamedTuple` under a synthetic
9204:   name:* wrapped both ways, the author writes `x.value` and gains nothing;
9205:   unwrapped into the bundle and rewrapped at the returns, the bijection is
9206:   owed at every seam and the synthetic name still surfaces in conditions,
9207:   readers, traces and messages.
9208: - *A `:return_type` arm on `ConformanceFailure`:* a declaration-reading
9209:   failure filed under [§9.5][s9-5]'s probed, fail-fast return law.
9210: - *A whole-value arm on `IllegalStateLeaf` or `IllegalStoreField`:* two
9211:   per-field contracts bent, and the fix the message states differs in kind.
