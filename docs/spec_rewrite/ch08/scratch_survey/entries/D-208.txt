7434: ### D-208 — Root inputs are the root component's input faces, whatever its class
7435: 
7436: **Status.** ratified
7437: 
7438: **Position.** The build accepts any component as root, and the model's root
7439: inputs are the root's input faces uniformly. The bundled rulings:
7440: 
7441: - For an assembly they are its `input_connections` keys, each traced
7442:   through the total face chain ([D-207][d-207]) to leaf input entries; for a
7443:   primitive, its `input_types` keys directly — [D-172][d-172]'s kind-blind faces
7444:   applied at the root.
7445: - Types keep their derivation (the ultimate consuming entry at the
7446:   activation scalar, [§8.2][s8-2]'s tight-bound rule; a primitive root's face is its
7447:   own consuming entry), and [§8.2][s8-2]'s primitive-root refusal is retired.
7448: - Abstract-at-root is unchanged: a primitive with an abstract input entry
7449:   still cannot be built bare, and the component test rig ([§13.7][s13-7]) keeps its
7450:   stubbing role, losing only its gate status.
7451: 
7452: **Spec.** [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.6][s8-6], [§9.1][s9-1], [§9.3][s9-3], [§11.3][s11-3], [§13.7][s13-7]
7453: 
7454: **Rationale.** The refusal existed because no rule made a primitive root's
7455: unfed inputs root inputs; the uniform doctrine supplies one with no new
7456: vocabulary, and the one-child wrapper it forced on leaf exercise added a
7457: level of ceremony without adding information.
7458: 
7459: **Rejected.**
7460: - *Keeping the assembly-only root:* with the doctrine uniform, the
7461:   remaining justification was the rig idiom, which survives on its own
7462:   merits.
