5818: ### D-167 — Mandate `Type{T}` input signatures under the permissive reading
5819: 
5820: **Status.** superseded → [D-263][d-263]
5821: 
5822: **Position.** `input_types(::C, ::Type{T}) where {T <: Real}` is **mandated on
5823: continuous consumers**, plain on discrete consumers, under the **permissive
5824: reading**: entries state per leaf what the consumer *allows* — `T` = tolerant
5825: (the activation scalar or a frozen `Float64` are both lawful arrivals; walking
5826: producers, frozen discrete producers and root slots all admissible —
5827: substitution intact), `Float64` = **demands frozen** (this input must never
5828: carry partials — the FFI door: a component whose internals cannot propagate
5829: `Dual`s declares it, its AD-incompatibility becomes schema-visible, and the
5830: failure moves from a `MethodError` inside user math at the `Dual` probe to a
5831: named wiring error at build), `Int`/`Bool` and abstract reference-typed entries
5832: (field handles) as today.
5833: 
5834: **Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.5][s8-5], [§13][s13], [§14.10][s14-10], [Appendix D][sD]
5835: 
5836: **Rationale.** Entries remain face bounds, not cell types: the nominal bound
5837: check is now "the producer's declaration evaluated at `Float64` <: the entry
5838: evaluated at `Float64`", unchanged in force.
5839: 
5840: Wiring acquires a second clause beside it, **evaluable in Stratum A at a marker
5841: scalar** (both sides are declaration functions of `T`; no user stage code
5842: runs): *for a continuous consumer, a walking producer leaf requires a `T`
5843: entry; a pinned producer leaf satisfies either* (frozen values embed upward),
5844: violation being `WalkingFaceAtFrozenEntry` (consumer path + entry, producer
5845: path + face, the leaf, both declared leaf types, both remedies in the message;
5846: [§13][s13]).
5847: 
5848: **The clause is tier-scoped, and the scope is decisive**: discrete
5849: consumers take the nominal bound check only, because their stages read
5850: exclusively at real ticks in the nominal world — a `Dual`-carrying cell exists
5851: only inside activations discrete stages never run in — so continuous → discrete
5852: wires are unconditionally legal; unscoped, the clause would reject the entire
5853: sensor → controller pattern.
5854: 
5855: Failure asymmetry, recorded: input-side forgotten-`T` (habitual `Float64` on a
5856: promoting input) fails **at first nominal build, at the wire, both endpoints
5857: named** — inputs have a build-time counterparty, outputs don't — with the
5858: didactic hint "if `C` promotes, declare the entry `T`".
5859: 
5860: Root slots: the slot type remains the entry at `Float64` (concrete tight bound,
5861: unchanged); slot cells walk by evaluating the entry at the activation's `T`,
5862: making **seedability schema-visible** — a `T`-entry slot is a lawful
5863: linearization `B`-matrix tap, a `Float64`-entry slot is declaredly unseedable.
5864: 
5865: The genericity obligation ("whatever scalars the wiring delivers, the
5866: consumer's math promotes") stays checked by the `Dual` probe, now scoped to the
5867: `T`-entries — a `Float64`-entry input imposes no such obligation, that being
5868: its point.
5869: 
5870: Escape from [D-033][d-033]'s rejection, recorded: the permissive reading predicts
5871: nothing and is not constant across components (pinned entries are rare but
5872: real), which is the reading the original adjudication never had on the table.
5873: 
5874: Annotation (2026-09-24): superseded by [D-263][d-263]. The mandate goes; the
5875: permissive reading, the walk-compatibility clause, its tier scope and the
5876: root-slot rules all stand, with `Pinned{P}` as the spelling of "demands
5877: frozen" and an unpinned `Float64` position as "tolerant".
5878: 
5879: **Rejected.**
5880: - *The predictive reading ("this is what will arrive") and the envelope reading
5881:   ("I promise to promote"), [D-033][d-033] upheld:* the predictive reading remains
5882:   rejected — producer-, tier- and activation-dependent, breaks substitution
5883:   behind a face; the envelope reading remains rejected — a universal
5884:   obligation, zero information.
5885: - *Symmetric `T`-forms on the discrete tier:* both directions
5886:   unfalsifiable-or-false: no execution path puts a `Dual` in a discrete cell,
5887:   and discrete reads never see one; if [§14.10][s14-10]'s sampled-data door opens, the
5888:   opt-in participation trait brings the `T`-signature with it — the hinge is
5889:   recorded here so the forms stay compatible.
5890: - *The unscoped wiring clause:* rejects every continuous → discrete wire;
5891:   caught in adjudication, recorded so the bug is never reconstructed as
5892:   intended semantics.
