9820: ### D-259 — Retire the strata: the build is three steps named by their products
9821: 
9822: **Status.** ratified
9823: 
9824: **Position.** The spec names the build's steps by what they produce and
9825: retires "stratum".
9826: 
9827: - The three steps are the structure step (the root instance in, `Structure`
9828:   out), the nominal evaluation (`Structure` in; `Dataflow`, `Events` and the
9829:   nominal `Float64` `Activation` out) and activation at a scalar
9830:   (`Structure`, `Dataflow` and the nominal activation in, `Activation{T}`
9831:   out). [§9.1][s9-1] opens with a table of what each step consumes and produces,
9832:   with the deployment and the materialization on it as the steps after the
9833:   build.
9834: - Each step is a barrier, as each stratum was ([§13.1][s13-1]). No user stage code
9835:   runs before the structure step completes. The nominal evaluation's
9836:   products are names only. Only activation re-runs per scalar.
9837: - The activation gloss is "the build's typed products at a given scalar
9838:   type". "Stratum A" reads "the structure step", "Stratum B" "the nominal
9839:   evaluation", "Stratum C" "activation"; [Appendix C][sC]'s group headings, [§9.6][s9-6]'s
9840:   heading and the glossary follow.
9841: - The log keeps its vocabulary of the day (`decisions_style.md` rule 2).
9842:   [D-253][d-253]'s fourth bullet is amended in place to the nominal evaluation's
9843:   return.
9844: 
9845: **Spec.** [§5.6][s5-6], [§6.1][s6-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.5][s8-5], [§9.1][s9-1], [§9.2][s9-2], [§9.4][s9-4], [§9.6][s9-6], [§13.1][s13-1], [§13.2][s13-2], [§13.3][s13-3], [§14.3][s14-3], [Appendix C][sC], [Appendix D][sD]
9846: 
9847: **Rationale.** The strata were named before any step existed as code. Once
9848: each step had a product ([D-253][d-253]), each letter had become a second name for
9849: that product: "Stratum A's product" for `Structure`, "a re-run of Stratum C"
9850: for an activation. A term that needs the product's name to be understood is
9851: the product's name. The letters also encoded a sequence the pipeline does
9852: not run. [D-253][d-253] as first written had Stratum C complete the nominal
9853: activation from B's stage-1 products, but `Events` is probed against every
9854: component's complete nominal product and the layout, so B runs the whole
9855: nominal chain, and a C run at `Float64` would be the separate pass [§9.1][s9-1]
9856: forbade. Stating each step by what it consumes and produces says that seam
9857: plainly: the nominal evaluation consumes one evaluation at `Float64` and
9858: fixes both the structure and the `Float64` typing, and activation consumes
9859: another scalar and fixes the typing at it. Everything the strata carried
9860: survives without the word: the barrier rule, the line between declaration
9861: reading and evaluation, and the line between structural and typed products.
9862: "Step" is the noun because the spec already uses it for deploying and
9863: materializing ([§9.2][s9-2]), and the integration step is always qualified
9864: ("integration step", "step boundary", `step!`).
9865: 
9866: **Rejected.**
9867: - *Keeping the letters with restated definitions:* the definitions would
9868:   have been the products' names with a letter in front.
9869: - *"Stage", "phase" or "pass" as the generic noun:* each already names
9870:   something (the stage functions, the sweep phases and the tick phase, the
9871:   declarative passes within a step).
9872: - *One function for the nominal evaluation and activation:* [D-253][d-253]'s
9873:   rejection stands; the nominal evaluation fixes structure and an activation
9874:   does not.
