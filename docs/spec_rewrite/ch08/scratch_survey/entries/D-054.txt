1543: ### D-054 — Producers determine activation types; consumers stay generic
1544: 
1545: **Status.** ratified
1546: 
1547: **Position.** Producers determine activation types, consumers accept — consumer
1548: obligation is genericity, checked by the `Dual` probe.
1549: 
1550: **Spec.** [§8.2][s8-2]
1551: 
1552: **Rationale.** Symmetric `T` on `input_types` is rejected as
1553: impossible-by-construction: an input's activation-time type depends on the
1554: producer's tier through the wiring (continuous → `Dual`, gated discrete → held
1555: `Float64`; consumers promote). The envelope reading (per-leaf genericity
1556: marking) is also rejected — zero information ([D-078][d-078]).
1557: 
1558: **Rejected.**
1559: - *`input_types(::C, ::Type{T})`:* forces the consumer to declare its
1560:   producer's tier; breaks on discrete-for-continuous substitution behind the
1561:   same face; [D-033][d-033]'s exact-`Float64`-face check was already the only coherent
1562:   consumer statement.
