10167: ### D-263 — One arity on both tiers: plain contracts, the `Pinned` marker and the mandatory store
10168: 
10169: **Status.** ratified
10170: 
10171: **Position.** Every contract declaration takes the component alone on both
10172: tiers, is written at nominal `Float64`, and is retyped by the framework. The
10173: tier is never spelled in a signature.
10174: 
10175: - On the continuous tier `input_types` and `output_types` are **walked**: the
10176:   leaf walk that types `init_x` and the root inputs is applied to the declared
10177:   types, so every `Float64` position follows the activation scalar. A leaf
10178:   wrapped as `Pinned{P}` is pinned at every activation, and the wrapper is
10179:   stripped at nominal. On the discrete tier the declarations pin wholesale,
10180:   as before, and a `Pinned` entry there is `DeclarationOnWrongTier`.
10181: - The permissive reading of `input_types` ([D-167][d-167]) stands with the marker as
10182:   its spelling: an unpinned position is tolerant, a `Pinned` one demands a
10183:   frozen arrival. Both wire clauses of [§6.1][s6-1] stand, decided at the marker
10184:   scalar by retyping the declarations instead of calling them.
10185: - The type walk substitutes `Float64` in type-parameter positions, yields `P`
10186:   at `Pinned{P}`, and does not enter a mutable type's parameters. A handle's
10187:   data references are fields and never walk; a handle with a scalar parameter
10188:   walks like any type, and `Pinned` freezes one built from build-time data.
10189: - `init_workspace(::C, ::Type{T})` keeps its scalar and takes it on **both**
10190:   tiers; the framework passes `Float64` to a discrete allocator at every
10191:   activation.
10192: - Every leaf declares exactly one of `init_x` and `init_s`, and a stateless
10193:   leaf declares it empty, `init_x(::C) = (;)` or `init_s(::C) = (;)`. The
10194:   store is the tier marker, mandatory even when empty, as `child_connections`
10195:   is the class marker ([§8.5][s8-5]). `StoreWithoutUpdate` applies to a non-empty
10196:   store only.
10197: - `TierSignatureMismatch` retires with all three arms. `TierUnreadable` names
10198:   a primitive declaring neither store, and an empty-store leaf with no
10199:   `output_types` is `StatelessWithoutOutputs`.

10201: **Spec.** [§4.3][s4-3], [§6.1][s6-1], [§6.2][s6-2], [§7.2][s7-2], [§7.3][s7-3], [§8.1][s8-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.3][s8-3], [§8.5][s8-5], [§8.6][s8-6], [§9.1][s9-1],
10202: [§9.4][s9-4], [§9.5][s9-5], [§13.7][s13-7], [§14.10][s14-10], [Appendix A][sA], [Appendix C][sC], [Appendix D][sD]
10203: 
10204: **Rationale.** The two-argument form charged every continuous leaf for a
10205: choice almost none of them make: the suite carries 11 pinned leaves against
10206: 192 `T`-form declarations, and a flight library's components promote by
10207: default because trim and linearization sweep whole assemblies. [D-166][d-166]'s own
10208: criterion says a `T` belongs in a signature exactly where the declaration's
10209: non-nominal behavior is underdetermined by its nominal restriction. With a
10210: leaf-level marker that underdetermination is gone: the plain declaration,
10211: `Pinned` included, fixes the declaration at every scalar, so the signature's
10212: `T` would record nothing the page does not already say. The retype walk is
10213: not new machinery. It is `retype`, the walk `init_x`, conditions and the
10214: root-input cells already go through; the contracts were the one declaration
10215: kind it did not reach, and carrying two conventions cost more than carrying
10216: one.
10217: 
10218: [D-166][d-166] reversed [D-079][d-079] on two grounds. Deliberate pinning of a real leaf was
10219: inexpressible under the plain form, and the plain form was reader-opaque,
10220: misleading to anyone not carrying the walk rule in their head. The marker is
10221: the element neither entry had on the table, and it answers the first ground
10222: outright: the pin is on the page, per leaf, schema-visible and
10223: conformance-checked, so [§14.10][s14-10]'s freeze door and the FFI door survive as
10224: declared doors. The second ground is revalued rather than answered. The walk
10225: rule is one rule, already taught for the state side; a reader who knows that
10226: `Float64` follows the scalar on the continuous tier reads every declaration
10227: in the framework with it.
10228: 
10229: The forgotten-`T` classes vanish rather than move. A `Float64` written at a
10230: participating leaf now walks, which is what the author meant. The class that
10231: takes its place, a forgotten pin on an FFI leaf, fails where the forgotten
10232: `T` did and never silently: an input pin is a wire check at the first
10233: nominal build only when written, and an output built from frozen data is
10234: refused at the first `Dual` activation by the identity comparison on the
10235: opaque leaf or by the `MethodError` inside the user's math, because no lossy
10236: `Dual → Float64` cast exists. The embedding guarantee ([§9.5][s9-5]) is untouched.
10237: 
10238: A stateless leaf loses its only tier signal with the arity, and the store
10239: supplies it. [§3.1][s3-1]'s continuous component has a state vector that may be
10240: empty, so a stateless continuous leaf is honestly spelled as a continuous
10241: leaf with zero state fields, `init_x(::C) = (;)`, and a sampled map as
10242: `init_s(::C) = (;)`. The empty NamedTuple is already the vocabulary's "no
10243: state": the bundle law puts a store's letter in the bundle only when the
10244: store is non-empty, so nothing downstream changes. Making the declaration
10245: mandatory is the move [§8.5][s8-5] already makes for `child_connections`,
10246: mandatory even when empty because defining it is the class marker; the store
10247: is the tier marker on the same terms. Every leaf's tier is then on the page
10248: in one place, stateful or not, with no tier by omission. The alternative
10249: that was on the table, an `is_discrete(::C)` trait defaulting to `false`,
10250: would have added a family name and a default, and it would have widened
10251: [D-178][d-178]'s recorded gap: an optional declaration lost to a local scope or a
10252: forgotten import silently drops its feature, so a sampler whose
10253: `is_discrete = true` was lost would silently become a transparent
10254: continuous wire, where a lost `init_s(::C) = (;)` fails loud as a leaf
10255: declaring no store. [D-173][d-173]'s and [D-178][d-178]'s rejections of the trait therefore
10256: stand. The cost is one line on every stateless leaf, the same line an
10257: assembly author already writes for `child_connections`.
10258: 
10259: The workspace keeps its scalar because its declaration is by allocation, not
10260: by type. Retyping a type is a pure substitution and total over the
10261: vocabulary; retyping an allocated value is undefined for a factorization, a
10262: plan or a buffer whose size depends on the scalar, which is [D-077][d-077]'s standing
10263: rejection of `similar`-based re-scalaring. The scalar is consumed by the
10264: allocation, so it is not ceremony in the contract sense. Taking it on both
10265: tiers removes the last arity-shaped tier fact: the discrete tier never runs
10266: off-nominal, so `Float64` is the only scalar its allocator can honestly
10267: receive; the ceremony falls on a rare declaration; and [§14.10][s14-10]'s sampled-data
10268: door would bring the scalar there anyway.
10269: 
10270: Opaque leaves need no stop of their own. The type walk substitutes
10271: parameters and never looks at fields, so a bulk-data handle whose references
10272: are fields passes through untouched, which is [§4.4][s4-4]'s frozen build-time data
10273: by construction. A handle with a scalar parameter is the case [D-237][d-237] admits, a
10274: different cell type per activation built at `T` by the stage; it walks like
10275: any type, and `Pinned` spells [D-237][d-237]'s other remedy. A mutable type's
10276: parameters pin by rule because no stage can produce a `Vector{Dual}` inside a
10277: handle without copying the grid at every evaluation, so no choice exists
10278: there for a marker to record.
10279: 
10280: Annotation (2026-09-25): the marker is read at the top of an entry alone, and
10281: one below it is `IllegalPortType` on both tiers ([D-265][d-265]); a frozen opaque leaf
10282: is admitted at a tolerant entry ([D-264][d-264]); the marker's meaning is "carries no
10283: partials", and an AD-opaque implementation that must participate supplies a
10284: local rule under a tolerant entry ([D-266][d-266]).
10285: 
10286: **Rejected.**
10287: - *Superseded position — the mandated two-argument forms on the continuous
10288:   tier ([D-166][d-166], [D-167][d-167]):* ceremony on every continuous leaf for a per-leaf
10289:   choice made on few; reader honesty bought at the price of a second typing
10290:   convention beside the state side's walk; the whole-signature and per-leaf
10291:   forgotten-`T` classes it had to contain exist only because the form exists.
10292: - *A one-argument `init_workspace` with framework re-scalaring:* [D-077][d-077]'s
10293:   grounds stand; reconstruction cannot cover plans, factorizations or
10294:   scalar-dependent structure.
10295: - *Keeping `init_workspace` tier-split:* one arity would stay a tier fact,
10296:   with its vote, its `DeclarationOnWrongTier` arm and a paragraph explaining
10297:   why this declaration alone still splits.
10298: - *A scalar-only marker (`PinnedFloat64`):* cannot pin an `SVector{3,Float64}`
10299:   or a handle whole; the parametric marker pins any leaf type.
10300: - *A parameter-position pin (`MyStruct{Float64, Pinned{Float64}}`):* fails a
10301:   `<: Real` bound unless `Pinned <: Real`, a lie in the type lattice; the
10302:   leaf-level pin covers every known case, and the limit is recorded.
10303: - *Stopping the type walk at every opaque leaf:* removes the handle-with-a-
10304:   scalar-parameter case [D-237][d-237] admits, and re-imports the inexpressibility
10305:   [D-166][d-166] charged [D-079][d-079] with.
10306: - *An `is_discrete(::C)` trait, default `false`:* a new family name and a
10307:   tier by omission; an optional declaration a local scope or a forgotten
10308:   import drops silently, turning a sampler into a transparent wire.
10309: - *Reading a stateless leaf's tier from its parent's `sample_times`:* a
10310:   block that is discrete by design, a `ZOH` or a sampler, could not say so,
10311:   and a user who forgets the entry gets a continuous wire with no ground for
10312:   the build to complain; substituting a stateless map into an unlisted slot
10313:   would silently flip its tier; the tier is the leaf's own fact ([D-056][d-056]).
10314: - *`nothing` as the empty store:* a sentinel return, the class [D-178][d-178] refuses
10315:   and `StoreNotNamedTuple` ([D-247][d-247]) already rejects; the empty NamedTuple
10316:   says the same thing inside the vocabulary.
10317: - *A stateless leaf with no outputs as a legal no-op:* admits a component
10318:   that produces nothing and stores nothing.
10319: - *Accepting `Pinned` silently on a discrete leaf:* a spelling that says
10320:   nothing is the class the declaration layer refuses.
