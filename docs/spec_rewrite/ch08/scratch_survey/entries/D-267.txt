10512: ### D-267 — Name the leaf declarations by the bundle field they define
10513: 
10514: **Status.** ratified
10515: 
10516: **Position.** Every leaf declaration that fills a bundle field is named by
10517: that field's letter and then a qualifier saying how. A declaration that
10518: defines no bundle field keeps its noun phrase.
10519: 
10520: - The renames: `init_x` → `x_init`, `init_s` → `s_init`, `init_m` → `m_init`,
10521:   `init_workspace` → `ws_init`, `input_types` → `u_types`, `output_types` →
10522:   `y_types`, `state_derivative` → `x_derivative`, `state_update` →
10523:   `s_update`, `state_projection` → `x_projection`, `output_state` →
10524:   `y_state`, `output_direct` → `y_direct`. Arities, signatures, bundle sets
10525:   and semantics are unchanged.
10526: - What stays: `state_events`, `sample_times`, `child_connections`,
10527:   `input_connections`/`output_connections`, `probe_value`, the binding and
10528:   device contracts, every bundle field, every `get_` selector and every
10529:   diagnostic kind name. The spec's formulas keep `f`, `g` and `h`, and prose
10530:   keeps "the state derivative", "the update law" and "the output stages".
10531: - [§8.1][s8-1]'s class 1 reads from here: a declaration is a noun phrase naming what
10532:   it returns, prefixed by the bundle field it defines where one exists.
10533: - No aliases and no deprecation shims, on [D-220][d-220]'s terms. The log keeps the
10534:   old spellings under `decisions_style.md`'s rule 2.

10542: **Rationale.** [D-263][d-263] left the tier with two signals, the store and the update
10543: law, and the old roster spelled them in different vocabularies: a letter in
10544: `init_s`, a word pair in `state_update`. A reader deciding a leaf's tier had
10545: to decode which state "state" meant. Under this entry a continuous leaf reads
10546: `x_init` and `x_derivative`, a discrete leaf reads `s_init` and `s_update`,
10547: and the tier is one letter repeated. A tier-disagreement diagnostic shows the
10548: disagreement in two letters, `s_init` beside `x_derivative`, where the old
10549: spelling made the reader decode it.
10550: 
10551: The old roster used three conventions for one idea. Stores took a verb and a
10552: letter, contracts took an English word and `_types`, laws and stages took the
10553: word prefixes `state_` and `output_`. One rule replaces them. Each
10554: declaration is named by the bundle field it defines, which is the left-hand
10555: side of its formula: ẋ gives `x_derivative`, s⁺ gives `s_update`, y gives
10556: `y_state` and `y_direct`, and the initial values give `x_init`, `s_init` and
10557: `m_init`. The rule is the subscript convention `implementation.md` already
10558: codifies for locals, symbol first and qualifier after, as in `n_x`, `t_seg`
10559: and `y_x`. Tab completion then groups by what an author is writing, `x_`
10560: listing init, derivative and projection together.
10561: 
10562: The grammar improves with it. A declaration is defined by the author and
10563: called by the framework, the class of `Base.size` and `eltype`, and those
10564: are nouns for what they return. `init_x` was an imperative, the grammar of
10565: `init!`. `x_init` reads "initial x", and every other name on the list reads
10566: the same way. [D-144][d-144]'s class 1 is stated as that noun rule from here rather
10567: than by enumerating prefixes.
10568: 
10569: The output stages take the shortest spelling of [§5.3][s5-3]'s own nouns, the
10570: "state-only output" and "direct feedthrough", and [§5.3][s5-3]'s reading that the
10571: absence of `direct` in the name is the no-feedthrough property survives
10572: unchanged. The pair stays tier-neutral, as [D-220][d-220] ruled, since the stages are
10573: machinery shared by both tiers. The "a state named y" misreading of `y_state`
10574: was weighed and accepted: `output_state` carried the same defect, and it
10575: fades once a reader knows `y` is never a store.
10576: 
10577: [D-220][d-220]'s grounds hold. Its shadowing argument needs the names to be
10578: distinctive as function bindings, and they are; `x_init` is common as a
10579: variable, but a module holding a value under that name refuses the method
10580: definition at its own line, loudly, so `DeclarationShadowed` gains no false
10581: positive. The one cost is a taken idiom: `x_init = x_init(c)` is a scoping
10582: error in a body that calls the declaration, and the suite names such locals
10583: `x0`. [D-220][d-220]'s objection to underscored suffixes was to variable suffixes,
10584: `h_x` and `h_xu`, which read as partial derivatives; a word qualifier carries
10585: no such reading. `ws_init` takes the bundle field because the rule says so,
10586: and `x_derivative` spells its word in full beside `x_projection`, under
10587: `implementation.md`'s full-word rule.
10588: 
10589: [D-076][d-076], [D-144][d-144] and [D-220][d-220] are annotated, not superseded. [D-076][d-076]'s `_types` suffix
10590: and its separation from `input_faces` survive, further apart than before.
10591: [D-144][d-144]'s four classes stand with class 1 restated. [D-220][d-220]'s choice of words
10592: over letters survives in the qualifiers, and its distinctiveness ground is
10593: preserved. [D-263][d-263] is untouched: the store is still the tier marker, under its
10594: new name.

10596: Annotation (2026-10-01): `x_derivative` is `x_deriv`, and the assembly's connection declarations leave the "what stays" list as `inner_connections`, `u_connections` and `y_connections` ([D-279][d-279]). The rule stands for leaves, and `u` and `y` also name the two sides of an assembly's contract. `x_projection` stays in full.

10598: **Rejected.**
10599: - *Keeping the verb-first stores beside letter-first laws:* the same
10600:   inconsistency in miniature, and an imperative is the wrong grammatical
10601:   class for a value declaration.
10602: - *`x_deriv`:* `get_deriv` is precedent, but the family otherwise spells its
10603:   words in full, and `x_deriv` beside `x_projection` is uneven.
10604: - *`x_dot`:* names the symbol rather than the quantity and reads as a value.
10605:   *`x_rate`:* collides with [§8.7][s8-7]'s rate scopes.
10606: - *`s_next`:* parallel to `x_derivative` in naming the result s⁺, but
10607:   "update law" is the spec's term throughout and `s_next` reads as a value.
10608: - *`u_contract`/`y_contract`:* the spec's word for the declarations, but
10609:   `_types` states the form the author must write, a NamedTuple of types.
10610:   [D-076][d-076] stands.
10611: - *`y_stage1`/`y_stage2`, `y_state_only`, `y_free`:* numbers state order,
10612:   not the dependence class [D-220][d-220] chose to name; the longer and vaguer
10613:   spellings lose more than the misreading they remove.
10614: - *Keeping `output_state`/`output_direct` as the one word-prefixed pair:*
10615:   leaves `y` the only bundle field whose declarations split between a letter
10616:   and a word.
10617: - *Renaming `state_events`:* the events define no single bundle field, and
10618:   "state" there names the detection class against time events ([D-220][d-220]).
10619: - *Aliases or deprecation shims:* [D-220][d-220]'s grounds.
