8665: ### D-236 — Type-check a wire by one relation with embedding, in Stratum A
8666: 
8667: **Status.** ratified
8668: 
8669: **Position.** A producer's declaration is accepted at an entry when some
8670: leafwise embedding of it, lifting any subset of its `Float64` leaves to the
8671: activation scalar, is `<:` the entry evaluated at that scalar. Both clauses of
8672: [§6.1][s6-1] are this relation, decided in Stratum A by evaluating the contract
8673: declarations at a marker scalar and collected under the stratum's barrier.
8674: - A concrete entry is checked leaf by leaf. An abstract entry is checked on the
8675:   whole declaration: the producer's declaration, or the same with every pinned
8676:   leaf lifted, must be `<:` the entry.
8677: - A root input's cells at an activation take the all-walking evaluation of the
8678:   root-input type when every consumer's entry admits it, and the root-input
8679:   type itself otherwise: [D-168][d-168]'s meet, at the level of the whole root input.
8680: - A root input whose entries are all abstract is `AbstractAtRoot`. Its concrete
8681:   entries must agree at `Float64` (`RootInputTypeConflict`); abstract
8682:   co-consumers are checked by the bound clause.
8683: 
8684: **Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§9.1][s9-1], [Appendix C][sC]
8685: 
8686: **Rationale.** At the nominal activation every lift is the identity and the
8687: relation is [§6.1][s6-1]'s bound check, `<:` with equality as the concrete case. Under
8688: a non-nominal activation the lifts express the walk-compatibility clause: a
8689: walking producer leaf must meet a `T` entry, and a pinned producer leaf meets
8690: either, because a frozen value embeds upward. So one relation is both clauses,
8691: and it is [D-166][d-166]'s embed-accept read from the input side. The abstract case
8692: cannot walk leaves. Its two candidates are exact whenever the entry's
8693: parameters are uniformly `T` or uniformly pinned; an abstract entry mixing the
8694: two against a producer pinning some of them is admitted by the definition but
8695: not by the check, a recorded limit with no known instance.
8696: 
8697: The meet on a root input is stated for the whole root input rather than per
8698: leaf, which is [D-168][d-168]'s letter. The two candidates decide it without a leaf
8699: walk and let abstract co-consumers vote. A mixture of pins across leaves pins
8700: the whole root input, the conservative direction, which never delivers a `Dual`
8701: where an entry forbade one.
8702: 
8703: Stratum A is where the check runs for two reasons. The contract declarations
8704: are functions of the scalar that construct types, so evaluating them at a
8705: marker runs no stage code. And the timing is the promise [D-167][d-167] makes: the
8706: input-side forgotten `T` fails at the first nominal build, at the wire. A check
8707: at the probe sees only what the current activation shows, so at nominal the
8708: walk clause is vacuous and the error waits for the first `Dual` activation.
8709: The marker pass evaluates `input_types` and `output_types` alone; `init_x` is
8710: by value and is walked, never evaluated.
8711: 
8712: Annotation (2026-09-24): the marker pass retypes the plain declarations at
8713: the marker scalar instead of calling two-argument ones; the relation and
8714: both clauses are unchanged ([D-263][d-263]).
8715: 
8716: **Rejected.**
8717: - *Equality modulo embedding, with nominal identity on struct types (the built
8718:   shape):* refuses an abstract entry's lawful concrete producer, so [§4.4][s4-4]'s
8719:   substitutability and [§8.2][s8-2]'s abstract entries have no code.
8720: - *The check at the probe, on the probed value's type:* one stratum late,
8721:   fail-fast where [§13.1][s13-1] collects, and blind to the walk clause at nominal.
8722: - *A per-leaf meet on root inputs:* legal by the clause but yields a cell type
8723:   no consumer declared, needs a leaf walk abstract co-consumers cannot join,
8724:   and goes beyond [D-168][d-168]'s wording for a case with no known instance.
