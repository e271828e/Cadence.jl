6073: ### D-173 — Fuse the discrete state letter `z` into `x`
6074: 
6075: **Status.** superseded → [D-195][d-195]
6076: 
6077: **Position.** The discrete state letter `z` fuses into `x`: `h_z`/`h_zu` →
6078: `h_x`/`h_xu`, `init_z` → `init_x`, `y_z` → `y_x`, bundle field `z` → `x`, `z⁺ =
6079: g(z,u,t)` → `x⁺ = g(x,u,t)`; `f`/`g` stay distinct (flow and jump maps); `m`
6080: and `Δt` keep their tier-scoped meanings; the per-function closed bundle-name
6081: sets become per-function-per-tier.
6082: 
6083: **Spec.** [§3.2][s3-2], [§5.2][s5-2], [§5.3][s5-3], [§7.3][s7-3], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.7][s8-7], [§9.3][s9-3], [§9.5][s9-5], [§13.7][s13-7], [§14.1][s14-1],
6084: [§5.3][s5-3], [§3.4][s3-4], [§8.6][s8-6], [Appendix C][sC] (all companions swept), [Appendix D][sD]
6085: 
6086: **Rationale.** [D-056][d-056] untouched and central — it is what makes the fusion
6087: safe, a leaf being strictly one tier and no component reading another's state.
6088: 
6089: Tier doctrine restated: stateful leaves declare tier by update law (`f` vs `g`,
6090: with `events`/`init_m`/`workspace`/`output_types`/`input_types` arities
6091: agreeing, [D-166][d-166]–[D-167][d-167]); a stateless leaf's tier is decided by its contract
6092: arities (`output_types` mandatory hence always the decider, `input_types`
6093: agreeing where declared) — no mere marker, the arity IS the tier's semantics
6094: (walking at `T` vs pinned, [D-166][d-166]–[D-167][d-167]) — and `DeclarationOnWrongTier` survives
6095: on the reduced member set while the wrong-letter error class dies with the
6096: distinction it policed.
6097: 
6098: Motivation: `z` collided with the shift operator (`z⁻¹`, five spec sites —
6099: [§13.7][s13-7]'s `UnitDelay` bullet had the letter meaning two things in three lines)
6100: and was nonstandard besides — discrete state-space writes `x[k+1] = g(x[k],
6101: u[k])` and hybrid-systems notation (Goebel–Sanfelice–Teel) writes flow `ẋ =
6102: f(x)` / jump `x⁺ = g(x)` with one letter, a convention the spec already
6103: inhabited (`f`/`g`, `x⁺ = project`); the fusion halves the stage/init surface;
6104: timing deliberate — increment 3 (the discrete engine) is unbuilt, so it is
6105: written once under the fused names.
6106: 
6107: **Rejected.**
6108: - *Renaming `z` to another letter:* fixes the collision only, keeps the doubled
6109:   API; every candidate letter is taken — `s` is the Laplace variable, the
6110:   mirror collision; `q` is spoken for by quaternions; `d` reads
6111:   disturbance/derivative — when every letter is taken the doubled scheme is the
6112:   problem.
6113: - *`is_discrete(::C)::Bool` trait as stateless-leaf tier decider:* derivable
6114:   hence redundant — restates what `f`/`g`, `events`, `init_m`, `workspace`
6115:   arity and `output_types` arity already fix, joining the
6116:   `DeclarationOnWrongTier` agreement set with zero new information; mandatory =
6117:   boilerplate on every leaf, defaulted = class-by-omission, which [D-039][d-039] refused
6118:   for kind; the corner it protects is rare — [§13.7][s13-7] steers stateless leaves
6119:   continuous — already netted by [§8.7][s8-7]'s rates-on-continuous error and [D-166][d-166]'s
6120:   Dual-activation CI policy, and behaviorally near-neutral under
6121:   tier-transparency; stays a cheap purely-additive retrofit if migration
6122:   falsifies the premise.
6123: - *Fusing `z` into `m` instead:* semantically tempting — discrete state is
6124:   latched memory — but it kills the method fusion, breaks the `x[k]`/`x⁺`
6125:   convention, and [§3.2][s3-2] already ruled the discrete tier's state IS its memory.
