10443: ### D-266 — Two doors for an AD-opaque implementation: the local rule and the `Freeze` block
10444: 
10445: **Status.** ratified
10446: 
10447: **Position.** `Pinned` records that a leaf carries no partials, not that an
10448: implementation cannot take them. An AD-opaque implementation has two doors
10449: and linearization keeps its one mechanism.
10450: 
10451: - A component that must participate in differentiation keeps its entry
10452:   tolerant and supplies its own local derivative inside the stage,
10453:   dispatching on the `Dual` type and rebuilding the output by the chain
10454:   rule ([§14.10][s14-10]).
10455: - A walking producer feeds a pinned entry through the `Freeze{V}` library
10456:   block ([§13.7][s13-7]), a stateless continuous leaf with a tolerant input and a
10457:   `Pinned` output that strips with `ForwardDiff.value`; the wire then
10458:   declares a zero coupling in every Jacobian along that path.
10459: - Linearization has the seeded `Dual` pass alone ([§14.10][s14-10]) and no
10460:   perturbation-based fallback.
10461: 
10462: **Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§13.7][s13-7], [§14.10][s14-10]
10463: 
10464: **Rationale.** A `Dual` can never become a `Float64` without discarding its
10465: partials, and [§9.5][s9-5] rules that no lossy cast exists inside the framework. The
10466: framework does not do the discard at a wire, on purpose, because dropping
10467: partials is a modelling decision the design wants on the page. The `Freeze`
10468: block is that page: at nominal `value` is the identity, so it costs one
10469: gather and one scatter; under a `Dual` activation it strips, and its output
10470: satisfies the pinned entry. A sampler is the other non-walking source, and it
10471: changes the model, holding the value between ticks and delivering it one
10472: sample late, which is right for a sampled consumer and wrong for a continuous
10473: value the consumer merely cannot differentiate through.
10474: 
10475: Where the coupling through the opaque component matters to the Jacobian, no
10476: adapter fixes it, and the local rule is the answer. Every stage is a pure map
10477: from its bundle to its outputs, so any such map, however opaque inside,
10478: admits a rule of one shape: evaluate at the value, obtain a local Jacobian by
10479: whatever means the component has, a finite difference at a step its own grid
10480: suggests or an analytic slope, and chain it onto the incoming partials. The
10481: stripping happens mid-expression, which [§9.5][s9-5] leaves legal at an unpinned
10482: leaf, and the seeded pass sees an ordinary differentiable component. The
10483: derivative is finite-difference in quality on that one component and exact
10484: everywhere else.
10485: 
10486: Trim keeps its derivative-free fallback ([§14.8][s14-8]) and linearization gets none,
10487: and the asymmetry is principled. Trim's fallback exists for problems with no
10488: Jacobian columns at all, the closed-loop sampled-data door where a frozen
10489: `state_update` gives the residual nothing to seed, and to keep the old
10490: algorithm reachable. Linearization has no analogous case: the discrete tier
10491: is frozen by definition and the continuous tier is always seedable.
10492: 
10493: **Rejected.**
10494: - *A perturbation-based linearization backend beside the seeded pass:* adds
10495:   no reach, since the local rule covers every opaque leaf; degrades every
10496:   linearization of every model containing one opaque component, silently
10497:   once the keyword is flipped, where the local rule puts the cost on that
10498:   component alone; reintroduces the global step-size heuristics the seeded
10499:   pass retired, with a perturbation that may cross a guard where a seed
10500:   cannot and attitude states that need a manifold-aware rule the seeded pass
10501:   gets for free. The one argument for it, a legacy model with a dozen opaque
10502:   tables linearizing on day one, loses because the rule is written once per
10503:   opaque type and a model that linearizes with silently degraded couplings is
10504:   the failure class the marker exists to prevent.
10505: - *The framework stripping partials at a walking-into-pinned wire:* the
10506:   discard is a modelling decision, and an invisible one is the class [§9.5][s9-5]
10507:   calls the silent zero in the Jacobian.
10508: - *Reading a pinned entry as the only door for an AD-opaque implementation:*
10509:   conflates "cannot take `Dual`s" with "carries no partials", and makes a
10510:   coupling that matters unreachable.
