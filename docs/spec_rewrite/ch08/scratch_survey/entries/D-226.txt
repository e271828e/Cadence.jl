8257: ### D-226 — Reach the public surface by qualified name until the export audit
8258: 
8259: **Status.** ratified
8260: 
8261: **Position.** A framework name is public by standing on [Appendix B][sB]'s surface,
8262: reached by qualified name or per-name `import`. Julia's `export` list is
8263: decided once, by the full-surface audit in `pending.md`, and until then the module exports
8264: nothing.
8265: 
8266: - "Exported" keeps two senses in the spec: an assembly face raised to its
8267:   boundary, and the exported-name audit. A function or name on the API surface is
8268:   "public". The rename sweeps the spec and its companions, not the log.
8269: - The interim is conformance, not a gap: the suite reaches every name through
8270:   `using Cadence` plus a per-name `import` list, [D-117][d-117]'s idiom, and a REPL
8271:   session loads the same list through `test/repl.jl`.
8272: - The authoring family stays unexported under [D-117][d-117] whatever the audit
8273:   decides for the operator half.
8274: 
8275: **Spec.** [§4.4][s4-4], [§9.4][s9-4], [Appendix B][sB], [Appendix D][sD]
8276: 
8277: **Rationale.** The 2026-09-04 conformance audit read "exported" at three sites
8278: as an obligation the module does not meet, `names(Cadence)` being
8279: `[:Cadence]`. The sites describe visibility. [§4.4][s4-4]'s value-level constructor is
8280: a function a model package must expose so condition math can call it outside
8281: any sweep; [§9.4][s9-4]'s `ProbeDual` is a name a CI activation list spells. Neither
8282: needs `export`; both need a public name. `pending.md` already records the export list
8283: as open, to be decided by a full-surface audit under the four-class
8284: convention, and names what the operator half carries: `condition`,
8285: `fragment`, `at` and `combine` share a namespace with FlightPhysics domain
8286: code, and whether the condition algebra ships behind a submodule is the
8287: packaging question. Exporting ahead of that audit would claim names it may
8288: rename or move. `step!` is exported by SciMLBase, `combine` by DataFrames and
8289: `mode` by StatsBase, and Julia settles such a clash at the first unqualified
8290: use, which is the by-accident outcome the audit exists to prevent. Nobody is
8291: inconvenienced meanwhile: the package has no external user, and the
8292: developer's session pays one `-L` flag.
8293: 
8294: **Rejected.**
8295: - *Export the operator surface now:* decides the list by accident ahead of
8296:   the audit `pending.md` records, and turns a later rename or submodule move into a
8297:   breaking change.
8298: - *Rule that nothing is ever exported:* forecloses the audit.
8299: - *Read "exported" as Julia's `export` and register the code as deviant:*
8300:   creates an obligation the design itself defers.
8301: - *A re-export submodule for the operator verbs:* the packaging `pending.md` lists as
8302:   open, half-decided.
