6247: ### D-177 — Re-found the periphery on mandatory roots plus declared traits
6248: 
6249: **Status.** ratified
6250: 
6251: **Position.** The periphery's declaration idiom is re-founded on declared
6252: traits over mandatory neutral roots: every device subtypes `AbstractDevice` and
6253: every binding `AbstractBinding`, mandatorily, so `attach!(sim,
6254: dev::AbstractDevice, binding::AbstractBinding; should_abort = false)` gates at
6255: dispatch — [§8.5][s8-5]'s one-root rejection does not transfer, the periphery's
6256: single-inheritance slot being vacant (no domain hierarchies compete for it) and
6257: a binding's sidedness being its **public contract**, not the [§8.3][s8-3]-hidden
6258: implementation class a component's tier is.
6259: 
6260: **Spec.** [§11.3][s11-3], [§11.6][s11-6], [§8.1][s8-1], [§8.3][s8-3], [§8.5][s8-5], Appendices A/B/C, glossary [§D.6][sD-6]
6261: 
6262: **Rationale.** Binding sides are **declared by Bool traits** with framework
6263: defaults on the root — `is_input`/`is_output` false, `is_greedy` false, the
6264: claim-source selector *within* the input side — and the enumeration obligations
6265: are enforced by **error fallbacks** (`claims(::AbstractBinding)`,
6266: `reads(::AbstractBinding)`) plus a **bidirectional conformance check at
6267: attach**: is_input && !is_greedy ⇒ `claims(b)` called once (a missing method
6268: fires the fallback loudly); is_input && is_greedy ⇒ claim computed and a
6269: defined `claims` method is an error; is_output ⇒ `reads(b)` called; neither
6270: side declared ⇒ attach-time error; is_greedy && !is_input ⇒ error; and a
6271: *specific* method of `claims`/`reads` defined for the binding type while the
6272: corresponding trait is false ⇒ error, detected by `which` against the fallback
6273: ([§8.1][s8-1]'s reflection class, run at a stopped-sim service point).
6274: Trait-true-method-missing and method-defined-trait-false both report the new
6275: diagnostic `BindingContractMismatch` (service), naming the trait and the method
6276: at fault. `map_input`/`map_output` remain conventions of the author-owned loop
6277: idiom the framework never calls — taught in [Appendix A][sA], never checked. What
6278: this closes: under method-presence detection a bidirectional binding whose
6279: `claims` was written *without* extending the framework generic (the [§8.1][s8-1]
6280: shadowing trap, one level down) degraded **silently to output-only** — the
6281: device attached, staked nothing and wrote nothing; `is_input = true` now makes
6282: the absence loud. The `sides(b)` rejection is **answered rather than ignored**:
6283: its ground was redundancy with methods that must exist anyway, and redundancy
6284: *with a cross-check* is drift detection, which is what the bidirectional check
6285: makes of it — annotated inline where [§11.6][s11-6] lists the rejection. Side-encoding
6286: supertypes stay rejected (a bidirectional binding cannot subtype two sides),
6287: and the empty-enumeration doctrine survives untouched: `is_input(b) = true` +
6288: `claims(b) = ()` is the honest may-write-nothing degenerate, while the maximal
6289: surface still requires the explicit `is_greedy` declaration.
6290: 
6291: **Rejected.**
6292: - *Presence-only probing, the prior design of record:* silent partial
6293:   degradation of a shadowed bidirectional binding, and `hasmethod` is the rarer
6294:   Julia interface idiom beside the AbstractArray/IndexStyle pattern of roots
6295:   plus traits.
6296: - *Optional, unenforced supertypes:* a signal half the ecosystem skips is worse
6297:   than no signal at all — the dispatch gate is the whole value.
6298: - *Side-encoding supertypes `AbstractInputBinding`/`AbstractOutputBinding`:*
6299:   unchanged from the standing rejection — a bidirectional binding cannot
6300:   subtype two sides.
6301: - *Annotation (from [§11.6][s11-6]'s inline treatment, migrated at the P3.2 rewrite,
6302:   2026-08-14):* under presence detection the degradation was not merely silent
6303:   but actively misdirecting — every diagnostic available pointed away from the
6304:   missing import.
