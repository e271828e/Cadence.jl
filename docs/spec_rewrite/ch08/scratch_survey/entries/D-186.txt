6595: ### D-186 — Legalize absolute declarations in any scope via anchors
6596: 
6597: **Status.** ratified
6598: 
6599: **Position.** Absolute declarations become legal in any scope — anchors,
6600: severing, and the derive-vs-declare rule: the `(T, τ)` pair an `Absolute` entry
6601: establishes is an **anchor**, severing its child from the enclosing scope's
6602: grid — no relation to the scope's ticks remains, so `K ≥ 1` reads "a child
6603: cannot tick faster than the scope it is *relative* to" (an anchored child may
6604: outpace its scope), the fastest-member convention counts relative members only,
6605: and anchored-vs-relative phase relationships are deployment-emergent, the
6606: printable bound schedule the way to audit them.
6607: 
6608: **Spec.** [§10.5][s10-5], [§9.1][s9-1], [§9.2][s9-2], [Appendix B][sB]
6609: 
6610: **Rationale.** The doctrinal line sharpened rather than dropped: an absolute
6611: declaration inside a library type is legitimate when the rate is **a fact about
6612: the modeled system, not a preference about the simulation** (a GPS's emission
6613: rate, a bus schedule, an ADC's conversion offset — forcing them to the root
6614: breaks encapsulation, the root re-declaring device internals), deployment
6615: preferences keep the exposed-multiplier idiom, and the framework cannot police
6616: the distinction — authoring doctrine, one paragraph beside the declaration
6617: forms; never-cache-`Δt` fully intact (the pinning sits in the enclosing
6618: assembly's `sample_times`, the same site the multiplier lives; the component
6619: type stays rate-agnostic and consumes the bundle's `Δt`); Stratum A compiles to
6620: **`(anchor, m, c)` triples** — seed `(A₀, 1, 0)` with anchor 0 the symbolic
6621: base grid, `Relative(K, φ)` step `(a, K·mₛ, cₛ + φ·mₛ)`, `Absolute` severs and
6622: re-seeds `(Aₖ, 1, 0)`, nested anchors just seeding again — the `Build` gaining
6623: the anchor and component tables with rate chain, final divisors for
6624: anchored entries deferred to binding (they do not exist until `Δt_base` does,
6625: and one `Build` backs many `Simulation`s); deployment: the **constraint pool**
6626: is every anchor period plus every nonzero offset, `Δt_base` admissible iff it
6627: divides the pool's GCD; **derive `Δt_base` only when every discrete component
6628: is anchored** — an unanchored period is `m·Δt_base`, and silent derivation
6629: would let an anchor edit anywhere rescale it, action at a distance — otherwise
6630: deployment must declare it, the refusal constructive; `Δt_base` binds from
6631: exactly one of three cross-validated sources — explicit keyword
6632: (`Rational`/`Period`/`Hz`, `n` derived and validated an integer ≥ 1), the `n·h`
6633: product, or derivation at the pool GCD — and resolution is one exact division
6634: pair per anchor (`DeploymentInvalid` naming the anchor's declaring scope and
6635: key when non-dividing) plus one multiply-add per component, the residue
6636: invariant surviving into the bound `(D, Φ, Δt)`.
6637: 
6638: **Rejected.**
6639: - *Root-only absolute declaration (the superseded [§10.5][s10-5] clause):* right about
6640:   deployment preferences, wrong for device-intrinsic rates; kept only as the
6641:   default-form rationale.
6642: - *Silent `Δt_base` derivation with unanchored components present:* the
6643:   logger-rescaled-by-an-offset-edit case.
6644: - *Absolute pinning from outside a subtree's contract:* standing rejection,
6645:   upheld.
6646: - *Any required relation between anchors:* they share only the base lattice,
6647:   which the grid derivation guarantees.
