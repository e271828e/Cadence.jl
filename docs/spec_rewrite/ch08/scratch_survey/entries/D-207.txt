7384: ### D-207 — Route every connection one level: faces are the only cross-boundary currency
7385: 
7386: **Status.** ratified
7387: 
7388: **Position.** Deep connection paths are removed: every endpoint in the three
7389: wiring declarations addresses an immediate child (container elements
7390: included) and one of its faces. Supersedes [D-009][d-009]. The bundled rulings:
7391: 
7392: - `child_connections` wires sibling faces, `input_connections` routes each
7393:   face to immediate children's faces, `output_connections` sources each face
7394:   from an immediate child's face — the generic-seam stop becomes the
7395:   universal rule.
7396: - The face graph is therefore total — every signal crossing an assembly
7397:   boundary bears a declared face there — and the `Build` retains it, input
7398:   side beside the output side, so per-level `at` addressing of a fragment's
7399:   `inputs` payload resolves (amending [D-065][d-065]'s "deep `at` only within owned
7400:   concrete subtrees" clause: a prefix stops at faces on every child, owned
7401:   or not).
7402: - Deep structural paths survive on the read side — inspection, logging,
7403:   the face routes — untouched.
7404: 
7405: **Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§8.6][s8-6], [§8.8][s8-8], [§9.2][s9-2], [§13.3][s13-3], [§14.2][s14-2], [§14.3][s14-3]
7406: 
7407: **Rationale.** The deep-path privilege made the face graph structurally
7408: partial: a root claim could cross intermediate boundaries without acquiring
7409: a face at any of them, leaving those levels blind to the signals traversing
7410: them — surfaced by the condition algebra, where
7411: `at("sub", fragment(inputs = …))` had no name to resolve against, and
7412: whether it could resolve depended on how an ancestor happened to spell a
7413: route. The privilege had meanwhile lost most of its value to the design's
7414: own growth: every level of a realistic tree is a generic seam ([§8.8][s8-8]), where
7415: one-level routing was already mandatory, and the worked assemblies route per
7416: level via `input_passthrough` and the single-authored feed list. Clarity and
7417: symmetry outrank convenience: the residual ceremony has sanctioned computed
7418: spellings ([§8.8][s8-8]), parallel routes proliferating through several boundaries
7419: are the signal to gather them into a custom type, and convenience remains
7420: recoverable a posteriori as sugar where clarity does not.
7421: 
7422: **Rejected.**
7423: - *Deep paths within owned assembly types ([D-009][d-009]'s position):* the bypass
7424:   is the one place structure leaks across a boundary [§8.3][s8-3] declares closed,
7425:   and the partiality it induces in the face graph is invisible from a
7426:   fragment author's seat.
7427: - *Keeping deep paths, retaining the partial face graph:* fixes the
7428:   literal `at` refusal; leaves resolvability an accident of ancestor
7429:   spelling.
7430: - *Bundled faces as the universal ceremony mitigation:* bundling serves
7431:   component-fed signal highways; at a root input it collides with [§4.3][s4-3]'s
7432:   write-side granularity and forfeits partial scripting ([§8.8][s8-8]).
