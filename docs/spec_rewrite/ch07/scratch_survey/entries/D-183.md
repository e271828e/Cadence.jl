6555	### D-183 — Retire workspace poisoning
6556	
6557	**Status.** ratified
6558	
6559	**Position.** Workspace poisoning is retired — the framework never inspects or
6560	mutates a workspace: it is an opaque, opt-in escape hatch used at the author's
6561	own risk, its rules stated as contract only (at call entry, contents are
6562	unspecified beyond structure the allocator established; scratch is garbage
6563	until written this call; no information carried between calls).
6564	
6565	**Spec.** [§7.3][s7-3]
6566	
6567	**Rationale.** `undef` allocation stays the recommended idiom and becomes the
6568	sole visible marker of meaningless contents; `PoisonSkip` retired; `debug`
6569	retired from `run!` (the poison was its only referent); supersedes [D-077][d-077]'s
6570	poison clause.
6571	
6572	**Rejected.**
6573	- *Scoped eltype poison of record ([D-077][d-077]: `NaN`/`typemin` + loud
6574	  `PoisonSkip`):* the poisonable/skipped boundary is an ill-defined
6575	  classification over opaque author-owned data (plans and factorizations are
6576	  valid from allocation; a recursive fill would corrupt them, the eltype
6577	  heuristic spares them only by coincidence), and a loudly-partial guarantee
6578	  still trains authors onto a net that may not be under them.
6579	- *Full-coverage poison:* not well-defined for arbitrary contents.
6580	- *Keeping `debug` as an empty hook:* speculative.
