505	### D-012 — Set-propagation tracers and SCC-based cycle diagnostics
506	
507	**Status.** ratified
508	
509	**Position.** Set-propagation tracers (global + sampled-local),
510	diagnostic-only; cycle diagnostics from SCC decomposition of the Stratum-B
511	topological stall (one SCC = one named loop), classified by a schedule-free
512	per-member local trace at the probe point (each member evaluated once in
513	isolation; in-cycle cells from `probe_value` under tracer tags) — real iff
514	every structural hop survives, artificial iff one dies; conservative on
515	discrete members (pinned signatures admit no tracer scalar), classification
516	optional on the error. Superseded on the verdict clause by [D-245][d-245] — over a
517	cluster the loop is real iff a traced cycle survives, which reduces to this
518	reading on a simple cycle.
519	
520	**Spec.** [§5.6][s5-6], [§9.4][s9-4]
521	
522	**Rationale.** Recorded only through the rejections below.
523	
524	**Rejected.**
525	- *Dual-based tracing:* derivative-zero blind spot.
526	- *Tracing as scheduling input:* soundness.
527	- *Naming the cycle by the topological stall's raw residue:* over-reports — the
528	  residue holds the innocent downstream cone.
529	- *Naming the cycle by a single DFS back edge:* under-reports — one edge of a
530	  possibly large tangle; both alternatives rejected, which is what the SCC
531	  decomposition buys.
