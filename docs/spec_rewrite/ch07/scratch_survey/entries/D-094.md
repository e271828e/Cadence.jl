2762	### D-094 — Close the state-leaf vocabulary to plain scalars and `SArray`s
2763	
2764	**Status.** ratified
2765	
2766	**Position.** State leaf vocabulary closed ([§7.1][s7-1]): `init_x` leaves are plain
2767	real scalars and `SArray`s at the common eltype — no domain wrapper types.
2768	
2769	**Spec.** [§7.1][s7-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§10.4][s10-4], [§14.3][s14-3]
2770	
2771	**Rationale.** Views therefore materialize by ordinary invariant-free
2772	construction (bit-faithful round trip, no constructor bypass, no
2773	layout-coincidence machinery in the executor), `Ẋ` has `X`'s shape at `T`, and
2774	off-manifold state is visible to [§10.4][s10-4]'s probes by construction; domain
2775	semantics are explicit casts at the point of use (`RQuat(x.q, normalization =
2776	false)` — today's `f_ode!`-over-raw-views pattern made immutable); invariants
2777	live in `project` and on the write paths (condition apply's baked converts
2778	[§14.3][s14-3], `capture`'s gather, handlers' own constructors).
2779	
2780	Annotation (2026-09-14): the declaration is also flat. A `NamedTuple` field
2781	would materialize without invariants, but the condition algebra and the
2782	readers address an `init_x` field as one leaf ([§14.3][s14-3], [§14.4][s14-4]), and structure is
2783	the component tree's to express; the check that enforces the vocabulary
2784	refuses nesting with the rest.
2785	
2786	**Rejected.**
2787	- *Invariant-carrying leaves with constructors run on read:*
2788	  `reconstruct(flatten(x)) ≠ x`: every consumer sees a silently projected value
2789	  over a runaway buffer — a clamped `Ranged` hiding its own divergence —
2790	  `project` view-redundant yet buffer-necessary, [§10.4][s10-4]'s off-manifold probes
2791	  impossible.
2792	- *Invariant-carrying leaves with constructor bypass:*
2793	  `reinterpret`/`unsafe_load` over the struct-buffer layout coincidence —
2794	  documented-C-layout-safe under the common-eltype rule and build-time
2795	  verifiable via `fieldoffset`, but internals-adjacent cleverness in the
2796	  executor core to save one cast line per method.
2797	- *Per-leaf lossless-round-trip certification:* an unverifiable convention, not
2798	  a checkable rule.
