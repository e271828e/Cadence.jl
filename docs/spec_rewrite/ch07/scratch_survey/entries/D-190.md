6828	### D-190 — Reject a separate `derivative_type` declaration
6829	
6830	**Status.** ratified
6831	
6832	**Position.** No `derivative_type` declaration: `Ẋ` has `X`'s shape at the
6833	activation scalar.
6834	
6835	**Spec.** [§7.1][s7-1], [§9.5][s9-5]
6836	
6837	**Rationale.** Derived from the closed leaf vocabulary ([D-094][d-094]) and the
6838	build-time flat layout — derivative completeness is a property of the layout,
6839	checked structurally at [§9.5][s9-5] (each field of `f`'s return scatters into its
6840	field's block at `T`), never of author discipline.
6841	
6842	**Rejected.**
6843	- *A per-leaf `derivative_type` override hook:* a second source for a fact
6844	  the layout already knows — the two can disagree, and the disagreement has no
6845	  adjudicator; the case that would motivate it, a leaf whose derivative lives
6846	  off its own type, is exactly what [D-094][d-094]'s closed vocabulary excludes.
