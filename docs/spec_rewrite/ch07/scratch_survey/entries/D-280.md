11668	### D-280 — Build a `Dual` activation of every component in CI
11669	
11670	**Status.** ratified
11671	
11672	**Position.** The repository's test suite builds a `Dual` activation of every
11673	component, with `build(world; activations = (Float64, ProbeDual))`.
11674	Linearizability is an invariant held by this policy.
11675	
11676	**Spec.** [§8.2][s8-2], [§9.4][s9-4], [Appendix B][sB]
11677	
11678	**Rationale.** Activations are lazy ([D-052][d-052]), so a successful `build` does not
11679	certify the model linearizable. A pinned `Float64` hidden in a constructor, or
11680	a misplaced `Pinned` leaf, lurks until the first `Dual` activation. The policy
11681	moves that first activation into CI. An activation is a re-run of the
11682	activation step alone, cheap per the kernel prototype ([D-166][d-166]). [D-166][d-166]'s annotation records
11683	that the policy survived [D-166][d-166]'s supersession by [D-263][d-263]. [D-099][d-099] spells the probe
11684	scalar, and [D-275][d-275] makes the keyword the whole entry point.
11685	
11686	**Rejected.**
11687	- *Eager `Dual` at every build:* doubles compile latency for a CI-only
11688	  guarantee. (As recorded in [D-052][d-052].)
