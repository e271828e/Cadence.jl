5771	### D-166 — Mandate `Type{T}` output signatures on continuous producers
5772	
5773	**Status.** superseded → [D-263][d-263]
5774	
5775	**Position.** `output_types(::C, ::Type{T}) where {T <: Real}` is **mandated on
5776	continuous producers**; plain `output_types(::C)` is **mandated on discrete
5777	producers**; both are Stratum-A-checkable (tier is known from declaration
5778	shape, [§8.5][s8-5]), so the whole-signature forgotten-`T` variant — the nastiest
5779	member of the class in the [D-033][d-033] world, where the plain form *was* the tier
5780	marker — is extinct by construction.
5781	
5782	**Spec.** [§7.1][s7-1], [§7.3][s7-3], [§8.1][s8-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.5][s8-5], [§9.4][s9-4], [§9.5][s9-5], [§14.10][s14-10], [Appendix D][sD]
5783	
5784	**Rationale.** Semantics are **literal**: cell types per activation = the
5785	declaration evaluated at that activation's `T`; the output-side leaf walk is
5786	deleted.
5787	
5788	Per leaf: `T` (alone or as a parameter, `MyStruct{T}`) = participating;
5789	`Float64` = deliberately pinned — **schema-visible whole-leaf freezing,
5790	delivering [§14.10][s14-10]'s recorded door** (declare `Float64`, strip with `value()` in
5791	the stage; the freeze is declared and conformance-checked); `Int`/`Bool`/ enum
5792	and reference-typed leaves as before.
5793	
5794	[§9.5][s9-5]'s embed-accept is re-keyed on declared-`T` leaves: a `T` leaf accepts
5795	exactly {activation scalar, `Float64`-embedded-as-zero-partial}; the
5796	constant-branch idiom (`flow > 0 ? f(x) : 0.0`) stays legal as written.
5797	
5798	An **observed `Dual` at a declared-pinned leaf** has one honest cause and gets
5799	the didactic hint: "if `F` participates in differentiation, declare it `T`".
5800	
5801	The residual per-leaf forgotten-`T` is a lurking-loud class (caught at first
5802	`Dual`-activation build, never silent — no lossy `Dual → Float64` cast exists),
5803	contained by an **adopted CI policy: the test suite builds a `Dual` activation
5804	of every component** (activations are Stratum-C re-runs; cheap, per the kernel
5805	prototype).
5806	
5807	Macro sugar over the declaration layer is a recorded door, compatible with
5808	[§8.1][s8-1]'s position (optional, on top, never required).
5809	
5810	`init_x`/`init_m`/`init_z` **stay one-argument**, and the criterion is
5811	recorded: **by-value declarations state nominal physics** (their *types* walk
5812	by rule — [§7.1][s7-1] forces every state leaf to follow the activation scalar;
5813	partials enter by per-invocation seeding, never initialization); **by-type
5814	declarations are functions of the activation scalar**; **by-allocation takes
5815	the scalar** ([D-077][d-077]'s `workspace(c, T)` precedent).
5816	
5817	Reversal grounds vs [D-079][d-079], recorded so it is never re-litigated from a worse
5818	memory: reader-honesty revalued (the sole author is also the six-months-later
5819	reader; the plain nominal declaration is misleading to anyone not carrying the
5820	walk rule in their head), and two of the three grounds that decided [D-079][d-079] had
5821	already dissolved independently — tier-by-declaration-shape removed the
5822	tier-marker trap, and embed-accept removed the constant-branch detonation that
5823	made the original `T`-form look fragile. [D-079][d-079]'s two conceded losses
5824	("schema-visible participation, whole-leaf stripping detection — tooling
5825	niceties") are re-valued as the point.
5826	
5827	Annotation (post-[D-173][d-173] re-examination, 2026-08-16): with `init_z` fused into
5828	`init_x`, the discrete tier also declares `init_x`, and the one-argument ruling
5829	was re-examined and holds — the discrete form stays plain by the same tier rule
5830	as `output_types`, and [§7.1][s7-1]'s ground is fusion-independent. The criterion's
5831	sharpest form, recorded so the next re-examination starts here: a `T` belongs
5832	in a signature exactly where the declaration's non-nominal behavior is
5833	underdetermined by its nominal restriction — by the per-leaf pin/walk choice on
5834	output contracts (this entry), by the tolerant/demands-frozen choice on input
5835	contracts ([D-167][d-167]), by vocabulary freedom on workspace ([D-077][d-077], which no walk
5836	rule could cover) — and nowhere else: [§7.1][s7-1]'s walk being total, a two-argument
5837	`init_x`'s output at any `T` is fully recoverable from its one-argument
5838	restriction, so its `T` would carry no information, only ceremony on values and
5839	a new forgotten-`T` class caught only at activation build.
5840	
5841	Annotation (2026-09-08): the per-leaf statement of embed-accept is decided on
5842	the type, by lifting and exact comparison, per [D-238][d-238].
5843	
5844	Annotation (2026-09-24): superseded by [D-263][d-263]. The literal semantics and the
5845	two-argument form go; the walk returns on the output side with a leaf-level
5846	`Pinned{P}` marker, the element this entry's reversal of [D-079][d-079] lacked. The
5847	embed-accept relation, the CI policy and the criterion recorded in the
5848	2026-08-16 annotation all survive; the criterion is what the marker
5849	satisfies at the leaf instead of the signature.
5850	
5851	**Rejected.**
5852	- *Plain declaration + leaf walk on the output side ([D-079][d-079]'s design):*
5853	  writer-honest — nothing to forget, no lurk — but reader-opaque, and
5854	  deliberate pinning of a real leaf inexpressible.
5855	- *The one-argument `Float64` fallback on continuous producers:* reopens the
5856	  whole-signature hole just closed; the "doesn't care about AD" population is ≈
5857	  empty in a flight library — trim and linearization sweep whole aircraft
5858	  assemblies — so the fallback mostly manufactures components that poison
5859	  model-wide `Dual` activations at someone else's first trim.
5860	- *Two-argument `init_x` by evaluation:* the `T` records no choice — [§7.1][s7-1]
5861	  admits no pinned state leaf; `zero(T)`/constructor ceremony on values; a new
5862	  forgotten-`T` class *on values* caught only at activation build; the didactic
5863	  side-by-side reading is bought instead by the recorded signature criterion.
5864	- *Two-argument `init_m`/`init_z`:* nothing could ever follow `T`; the
5865	  signature would invite exactly the misplacement [§8.2][s8-2]'s didactic errors exist
5866	  to catch.
5867	- *Blanket `T`-signatures on all declarations:* ritual dilutes the signal — the
5868	  reader-honesty of `T` depends on its appearing exactly where a choice was
5869	  made; the criterion, not uniformity, is the rule.
