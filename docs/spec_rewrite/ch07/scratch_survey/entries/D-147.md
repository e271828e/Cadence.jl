5017	### D-147 — Split the sweep into static interior and boundary variants
5018	
5019	**Status.** ratified
5020	
5021	**Position.** The sweep is two statically distinct variants compiled from one
5022	entry list, because discreteness is a build-time fact and the split by
5023	discreteness is therefore static rather than a runtime gate. The interior sweep
5024	walks continuous entries only and is what RK stage evaluations and localization
5025	guard probes run, so the mid-step ZOH holds by construction ([D-019][d-019]'s ZOH
5026	semantics reaffirmed) — discrete entries are not gated out at runtime, they are
5027	absent from the walk at compile time, and the hottest path in the framework
5028	carries no gating test at all. The boundary sweep walks the full list, with
5029	discrete entries gated by counter modulo against the boundary's tick index —
5030	the pre-existing [§10.5][s10-5] mechanism, now explicitly scoped to boundaries, the only
5031	place anything discrete ever happens ([§10.6][s10-6]'s macro-sequence is its sole
5032	caller). Both blocks split identically: discrete `h_z` entries are as absent
5033	from the interior `h_x` walk as discrete `h_zu` entries are from the interior
5034	`h_xu` walk. Phase-body surface, amending [D-116][d-116]: the two sweep bodies gain an
5035	arity distinction — zero-arg `sweep_hx()`/`sweep_hxu()` are the interior
5036	variants, which is what makes `@ballocated(sweep_hxu()) == 0` a well-defined
5037	measurement of the interior path rather than of whichever tick phase the
5038	simulation happens to be sitting in, while `sweep_hx(tick)`/`sweep_hxu(tick)`
5039	are the boundary variants gating by modulo against the passed index, symmetric
5040	with `ticks(tick)`; `rhs` is unchanged, and each arity is asserted in its own
5041	right at the CI seam. The due set is a property of the boundary, computed once
5042	and reused by every re-sweep of its quiescence iteration (a due component is at
5043	its tick instant for the whole boundary, not for one round of it): the
5044	counter-modulo image of the frame index at a frame top, empty at `t*` — the
5045	tick counter has not advanced there, so no component is at a tick instant and a
5046	naive modulo test against the unadvanced index would wrongly re-admit the
5047	previous frame's due set — and everything at boundary zero, which is [§14.5][s14-5]'s
5048	existing rule (`t₀` is a grid point of every divisor, and no earlier tick
5049	exists for a ZOH to hold) rather than a new one.
5050	
5051	**Spec.** [§5.3][s5-3], [§10.3][s10-3], [§10.4][s10-4], [§10.5][s10-5], [§10.6][s10-6], [§9.7][s9-7], [§14.5][s14-5], [Appendix D][sD]
5052	
5053	**Rationale.** The split also recovers an implicit commitment: [§9.7][s9-7]'s
5054	recompilation-granularity sentence — editing a discrete component invalidates
5055	the boundary body, not the RHS body — is true only under a two-body split, and
5056	was quietly falsified by the single-mechanism reading.
5057	
5058	Annotation (2026-10-02): amended by [D-185][d-185], [D-205][d-205] and [D-291][d-291] for
5059	boundary zero, as on [D-067][d-067], and renamed by [D-196][d-196]: `sweep_hx` and
5060	`sweep_hxu` are `sweep_1` and `sweep_2`.
5061	
5062	**Rejected.**
5063	- *Superseded position — counter-modulo gating as the sole mechanism (the prior
5064	  single-mechanism reading, and the finding):* at divisor 1 — the common `n = K
5065	  = 1` configuration — the test admits every discrete entry at every
5066	  evaluation, so an `h_zu` re-runs against interpolated `u` at each interior RK
5067	  stage and at every localization probe, and the `f` reading its cell
5068	  integrates a continuously re-sampled controller — precisely the un-sampling
5069	  [§10.5][s10-5]'s own rejected alternative names, and undiagnosable: such a run is as
5070	  type-stable, allocation-free and bit-reproducible against itself as the
5071	  correct one, so a C172X at `h = 0.02, n = 1` under a 50 Hz FCS simply flies a
5072	  different trajectory with nothing anywhere to flag it.
5073	- *A single parameterized body taking a sentinel "no ticks due" index* on
5074	  mid-step calls: identical semantics, rejected on three counts — it pays a
5075	  dead runtime test per discrete entry in the framework's hottest path, where
5076	  the static split pays nothing at all; it puts discrete entries back into the
5077	  RHS-side body, falsifying [§9.7][s9-7]'s recompilation-granularity claim; and it
5078	  conflates two execution contexts inside one measured body, so `@ballocated`
5079	  at the seam measures whichever context the sentinel selects and the
5080	  zero-allocation canary loses its per-path meaning.
5081	- *Reading the tick index out of simulation state inside an unparameterized
5082	  `sweep_hxu()`:* [D-116][d-116]'s surface read literally — the accessor can then no
5083	  longer express "the interior variant", and the measurement silently depends
5084	  on the tick phase the simulation happens to be paused at.
