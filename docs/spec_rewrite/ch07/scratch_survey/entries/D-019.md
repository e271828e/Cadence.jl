672	### D-019 — Harmonic tick grid with virtual assemblies and rate scopes
673	
674	**Status.** ratified
675	
676	**Position.** Harmonic tick grid on step boundaries; discrete stages gated to
677	own tick instants (ZOH by construction); assemblies virtual for execution, rate
678	scopes for declaration (integer multipliers $K \ge 1$ composing down the tree,
679	compiled to absolute divisors); `Δt` arrives as a discrete-bundle field
680	([D-074][d-074]), single source of truth, no stored `Δt`-derived parameters.
681	
682	**Spec.** [§5.4][s5-4], [§10.5][s10-5]
683	
684	**Rationale.** Recorded only through the rejections below.
685	
686	Annotation (2026-10-02): amended by [D-185][d-185], [D-186][d-186] and [D-283][d-283]. Phases are
687	adopted, reversing the phase-offset rejection below, and rates compile to
688	anchor-relative triples whose divisors deployment binds.
689	
690	**Rejected.**
691	- *Superseded position — a `comp.Δt` virtual property (the earlier design of
692	  record):* impossible, because `===`-identical siblings can sit under
693	  different `rates` keys, so the period is a schedule-position fact, not a
694	  component fact ([D-074][d-074]).
695	- *Atomic assemblies, incl. opt-in:* coarsened schedulable unit → [§5.4][s5-4]
696	  artificial loops at assembly scale; interleaving protection meaningless under
697	  the signal table; FlightCore's whole-tree atomicity was a call-tree artifact.
698	  The atomic-assembly census behind that rejection: Simulink documents the
699	  artificial-loop hazard for its Atomic Subsystems, and the construct's other
700	  roles there — code-generation units, enabled/triggered execution — have no
701	  counterpart in the consumers, conditional behavior ("on ground, force
702	  `direct`") being mode logic.
703	- *Arbitrary tick periods via time queue:* variable `h`, irregular frames, no
704	  demonstrated need.
705	- *Absolute-period declaration as default:* welds deployment rates into
706	  reusable designs; base-period variables don't compose across independently
707	  authored assemblies.
708	- *Re-running discrete stages every boundary:* un-samples sampled-data
709	  semantics.
710	- *Phase offsets:* no demonstrated use.
711	- *`Δt` via `h`-argument only:* discretized laws live in the feedthrough stage.
