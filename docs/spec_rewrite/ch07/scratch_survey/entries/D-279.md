11575	### D-279 — Name the assembly's boundary declarations by `u` and `y`, and shorten `x_derivative`
11576	
11577	**Status.** ratified
11578	
11579	**Position.** The letters `u` and `y` name the two sides of a component's
11580	contract at either class, and four declarations and one keyword are renamed
11581	on that ground or beside it.
11582	
11583	- The renames: `input_connections` → `u_connections`, `output_connections` →
11584	  `y_connections`, `child_connections` → `inner_connections`, `x_derivative`
11585	  → `x_deriv`, and `fragment`'s `inputs` keyword → `u`. Signatures, entry
11586	  spellings and semantics are unchanged.
11587	- [D-267][d-267]'s rule is widened, not replaced. A leaf declaration is still named
11588	  by the bundle field it defines. An assembly has no bundle, and its boundary
11589	  declarations take the letter of the contract side they declare.
11590	- What stays: `x_projection`, `state_events`, `sample_times`, `Group`'s
11591	  keywords `wires`, `inputs` and `outputs`, `input_faces`/`output_faces`,
11592	  `input_passthrough`/`output_passthrough`, every `get_` selector and every
11593	  diagnostic kind name. Prose keeps "root input", "the state derivative" and
11594	  "the projection".
11595	- `deriv` joins `implementation.md`'s abbreviation roster.
11596	- No aliases and no deprecation shims, on [D-220][d-220]'s terms. The log keeps the
11597	  old spellings under `decisions_style.md`'s rule 2.

11604	**Rationale.** [§8.6][s8-6] already says that the root's input faces come from the
11605	assembly's boundary declaration or from `u_types`, following the root's
11606	class, and that nothing downstream distinguishes the two ([D-208][d-208]). Two
11607	declarations that fill one role at the two classes were spelled in two
11608	vocabularies. Under this entry `u_types` and `u_connections` read as the two
11609	ways a component declares its input side, and `u_` lists both under tab
11610	completion. Control convention gives a composite system a `u` and a `y` as
11611	it gives them to a block. [D-267][d-267] kept the three connection names because an
11612	assembly defines no bundle field. That is still true. The letter names the
11613	contract side, which on a leaf is also the bundle field. The suffix keeps
11614	the difference [D-041][d-041] and [D-170][d-170] drew: `_types` declares typed faces, and
11615	`_connections` routes faces whose types derive from the internal endpoints.
11616	
11617	`fragment` took three letters and one word. Its fourth payload holds values
11618	for the fields of `u`, so the letter fits. `x`, `s` and `m` resolve only at a
11619	leaf, while this payload resolves at any level, assemblies included. The
11620	keyword is therefore clean only because the boundary rename makes `u` mean
11621	something on an assembly.
11622	
11623	[D-170][d-170] rejected `inner_connections` for its visual near-collision with
11624	`input_connections`. That neighbour is gone. Every entry of all three
11625	declarations names a child face, so "child" told none of them apart. "Inner"
11626	says that both ends sit inside the boundary, against one end on it. The trio
11627	is uneven in form, one word and two letters, and the form is accurate: the
11628	first is the class marker with no leaf counterpart, and the other two mirror
11629	`u_types` and `y_types`.
11630	
11631	[D-267][d-267] rejected `x_deriv` on two grounds, and neither holds. The family does
11632	not spell its words in full: `x_init`, `s_init`, `m_init` and `ws_init`
11633	abbreviate already. `x_deriv` beside `x_projection` is no more uneven than
11634	`x_init` beside it. The read side already says `get_deriv`, so the
11635	declaration and its selector now spell one word one way. It is also the
11636	declaration authors write most, once per continuous leaf. "Deriv" could be
11637	misread as "derived", a word the spec uses for contracts, but `get_deriv`
11638	had fixed its meaning before this entry.

11640	Annotation (2026-10-02): partly superseded by [D-303][d-303]. The three connection
11641	names fall, with the widening of [D-267][d-267]'s rule to an assembly's boundary
11642	declarations that produced two of them. The declarations are `inner_wires`,
11643	`input_wires` and `output_wires`. The listing of `Group`'s keywords among
11644	what stays falls too, since those keywords and fields now take the
11645	declaration names and `sample_times`. `x_deriv`, the `fragment` keyword `u`
11646	and the rejection of bare `wires` as a function name stand. The keyword now
11647	rests on [§8.6][s8-6] alone, because its ground in the `u_connections` name is gone.
11648	
11649	**Rejected.**
11650	- *`sibling_connections`:* [§6.1][s6-1]'s own adjective for these wires, but a child
11651	  wired to itself is expressible (`"plant/power" => "plant/u"`), and a child
11652	  is not its own sibling.
11653	- *`wires`:* the spec's everyday noun and `Group`'s field, but it drops the
11654	  `_connections` suffix that ties the three declarations, and a package
11655	  function of that name would take it from every `wires` local and from the
11656	  `Group` field under `implementation.md`'s naming rules.
11657	- *Keeping `child_connections` beside `u_connections` and `y_connections`:*
11658	  the name discriminates nothing, since all three declarations connect child
11659	  faces.
11660	- *`x_proj`:* `proj` is neither frequent nor established. Few leaves write a
11661	  projection, tab completion spares the writer, and a reader who meets the
11662	  name rarely is better served by the full word.
11663	- *`u` and `y` as `Group`'s keywords:* they hold connection entries, not
11664	  values, and `u = ("in" => "c/e",)` would read as a value of `u`.
11665	- *Letters on the helpers and selectors:* [D-267][d-267] separates declarations from
11666	  these, and [D-076][d-076]'s separation of `u_types` from `input_faces` stands.
