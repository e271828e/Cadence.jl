11967	### D-288 — The executor's structure: phase bodies, views, construction, the gate and publication
11968	
11969	**Status.** ratified
11970	
11971	**Position.** The executor's structure is fixed in five parts, and two
11972	options it opens stay uncommitted.
11973	
11974	- Phase bodies are the outer decomposition, and they are semantically
11975	  forced: the order-free stage-1, `x_deriv` and `s_update` blocks, the
11976	  topologically ordered stage-2 block, and the event callables.
11977	- Views are rebuilt per call. Hoisting is the code generator's
11978	  common-subexpression elimination, and the framework maintains none.
11979	- Construction is type-opaque. Entry tuples are built from untyped buffers,
11980	  and the compiled tuple's type has one consumer, the unrolled walk.
11981	- Two options are recorded, not committed: deterministic parallel
11982	  evaluation of the order-free blocks, and finer recompilation granularity.
11983	  Editing a discrete component already invalidates the boundary body, not
11984	  the RHS body, under the two-arity split.
11985	- One gate serves the three tick-sensitive blocks, because due-ness is per
11986	  component, per boundary, never per stage. At a localized event time `t*`
11987	  the empty due set is arity selection.
11988	- Publication is not a phase body. Invoking phase bodies in isolation leaves
11989	  the buffers valid but off-trajectory, and continuing takes `init!`.
11990	
11991	**Spec.** [§9.7][s9-7], [§10.5][s10-5]
11992	
11993	**Rationale.** The phase bodies are the semantically forced outer
11994	decomposition, and their seams cost nothing; views rebuild per call, hoisting
11995	being compiler CSE, whose legality condition is the staleness rule; schedule
11996	tuples are constructed type-opaquely and consumed only by the walk; and the
11997	parallel-evaluation and incremental-recompile doors are recorded (as recorded
11998	in [D-086][d-086]). [D-086][d-086]'s chunking and mitigation ladder are not restated here: they
11999	wait on the compile-time ruling in `pending.md`. That a discrete edit
12000	invalidates the boundary body and not the RHS body is an implicit commitment
12001	the two-body split makes true (as recorded in [D-147][d-147]).
12002	
12003	The boundary gate is one gate for all three tick-sensitive blocks, due-ness
12004	per component per boundary and never per stage, and `t*` emptiness remains
12005	arity selection, with no sentinel index failing every gate (as recorded in
12006	[D-185][d-185]). [D-147][d-147] rules the due set empty at `t*` and the two arities.
12007	
12008	Publication is not a phase body, which is the [§7.5][s7-5] carve-out made
12009	structural; isolated invocation leaves buffers valid but off-trajectory, and
12010	re-`init!` continues (as recorded in [D-116][d-116]).

12016	**Rejected.**
12017	- *Framework-maintained view hoisting:* manual cache-invalidation duty for
12018	  loads the compiler hoists exactly where legal; mis-scoping = silent
12019	  stale-state reads. (As recorded in [D-086][d-086].)
12020	- *A single parameterized body taking a sentinel "no ticks due" index on
12021	  mid-step calls:* it pays a dead runtime test per discrete entry in the
12022	  framework's hottest path; it puts discrete entries back into the RHS-side
12023	  body, falsifying [§9.7][s9-7]'s recompilation-granularity claim; and it conflates
12024	  two execution contexts inside one measured body. (As recorded in [D-147][d-147].)
12025	- *A no-publish advance mode on `step!`:* forks execution semantics for CI's
12026	  benefit and breaks [§10.1][s10-1]'s publication-after-every-boundary property. (As
12027	  recorded in [D-116][d-116].)
12028	- *None recorded for type-opaque construction or the two uncommitted
12029	  options.*
