9238	### D-247 — Accept the `NamedTuple` as the only store declaration form
9239	
9240	**Status.** ratified
9241	
9242	**Position.** Every by-value store declaration, `init_x`, `init_s` and
9243	`init_m`, returns a `NamedTuple` with one named field per leaf, and no other
9244	form is admitted. Stratum A checks the form on every primitive before the
9245	tier classifier and the vocabulary checks read the value, and reports a bare
9246	value as `StoreNotNamedTuple`, collected, carrying the store, the observed
9247	type and the wrap that fixes it.
9248	
9249	**Spec.** [§7.1][s7-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§9.1][s9-1], [Appendix C][sC]
9250	
9251	**Rationale.** Nothing checked the form, and every reader of a store value
9252	assumes a `NamedTuple`: the tier classifier, the two Stratum A field checks,
9253	`Decls`, the leaf walk and the bundle. Probed on Julia 1.13.0 with leaves
9254	sound otherwise, a `Float64` or an `SVector` from `init_x` and an `Int` from
9255	`init_s` failed as a raw `MethodError` converting to `NamedTuple` in Stratum
9256	C; a `Tuple` from `init_x` failed inside the generated `reconstruct` and one
9257	from `init_s` as a `BoundsError`; a `Vector` from `init_x` segfaulted in the
9258	generated `reconstruct`; a `Symbol` from `init_m` failed as
9259	`isempty(::Symbol)` in the classifier; and an `Int` from `init_m` built
9260	silently as the mode store. The field checks read `pairs(1.0)` without
9261	complaint, so the first refusal came from a stratum that runs user code, or
9262	from the compiler.
9263	
9264	The bare leaf was weighed as an ergonomic form for one-state components and
9265	rejected on where the field name does work. Every service reaches a leaf by
9266	its field name: the condition overlay is a `NamedTuple` merge keyed on it
9267	([§14.1][s14-1]), readers and the trace header spell `plant/x.q` ([§11.5][s11-5], [§14.4][s14-4]), the
9268	trim problem's decisions and residuals name it ([§14.7][s14-7]), auto-publication
9269	publishes a state field as the port of the same name ([§5.3][s5-3]), the return laws
9270	pair by it ([§9.5][s9-5]), and the payloads of `IllegalStateLeaf` and
9271	`ConformanceFailure` carry it. A bare leaf would be a second store shape at
9272	each of those seams and would forfeit auto-publication, having no name to
9273	match. The name a one-state component's author is asked for is the name every
9274	service then uses, the discipline `output_types` already asks of ports. The
9275	leaf walk is not the obstacle: an `SArray` lays out wherever it sits.
9276	
9277	The check collects under [§13.1][s13-1]'s rule for declarative checks and runs first
9278	in the walk's primitive branch, since the classifier and the field checks
9279	cannot read the value. A primitive that fails it records `nothing` for its
9280	tier and is read no further in Stratum A. An empty `(;)` is the fallback's
9281	own value and keeps reading as "declares nothing".
9282	
9283	Annotation (2026-09-25): the empty store `(;)` is a `NamedTuple` and admitted; it
9284	is the stateless leaf's mandatory tier marker ([D-263][d-263]).
9285	
9286	**Rejected.**
9287	- *A single leaf as a second store form, addressed as the store itself:* a
9288	  second shape at every name-keyed seam (conditions, readers, trace, trim,
9289	  auto-publication, the return laws, the payloads), no auto-publication for
9290	  it, and no grounding model that wants it, the case studies carrying several
9291	  leaves per component.
9292	- *Canonicalizing a bare value into a one-field `NamedTuple` under a synthetic
9293	  name:* wrapped both ways, the author writes `x.value` and gains nothing;
9294	  unwrapped into the bundle and rewrapped at the returns, the bijection is
9295	  owed at every seam and the synthetic name still surfaces in conditions,
9296	  readers, traces and messages.
9297	- *A `:return_type` arm on `ConformanceFailure`:* a declaration-reading
9298	  failure filed under [§9.5][s9-5]'s probed, fail-fast return law.
9299	- *A whole-value arm on `IllegalStateLeaf` or `IllegalStoreField`:* two
9300	  per-field contracts bent, and the fix the message states differs in kind.
