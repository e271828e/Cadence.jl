6281	### D-176 — Unify trace retention on one sparse record format
6282	
6283	**Status.** ratified
6284	
6285	**Position.** Trace retention unifies on one sparse record format (supersedes
6286	[D-107][d-107]'s density rule): the verbatim-vs-sparse dichotomy is eliminated —
6287	**every** writer's drained batch is sparse-converted at the drain into
6288	(position ⇒ value) pairs against the header's schema, so the trace has one
6289	consumer-facing record format, no per-entry format flag, and replay one
6290	inverse-conversion path.
6291	
6292	**Spec.** [§7.5][s7-5], [§11.5][s11-5], [§12.7][s12-7], [Appendix D][sD]
6293	
6294	**Rationale.** Costs recorded where they are paid: ~2× trace size on the dense
6295	component (two small values per touched face where the tuple held one, the
6296	order unchanged and [D-029][d-029]'s two-orders-below-the-log budget untouched); the
6297	conversion allocates once per drained batch, in-class with [§7.5][s7-5]'s retention
6298	carve-out and smaller than the log's per-boundary snapshot, which the carve-out
6299	already covers. The decision is **reversible as pure implementation**:
6300	conversion is lossless both ways, so verbatim retention could return as a
6301	per-entry storage optimization without touching the record semantics, the
6302	header or replay. Motivation: [D-174][d-174] made "enumerated ⇒ claim-narrow ⇒ dense"
6303	false — a greedy claim is enumerated and as wide as the root contract — so
6304	density could only be re-keyed on the claim source, i.e. on a distinction [D-174][d-174]
6305	exists to erase.
6306	
6307	**Rejected.**
6308	- *Keying retention by claim source:* a dichotomy needing re-justification at
6309	  every change to the surface machinery, resting on a premise — enumerated
6310	  means narrow — that `is_greedy` falsifies.
6311	- *Deciding density per batch at the drain:* per-frame heterogeneous record
6312	  formats, a format flag on every entry and two decoders in every consumer, for
6313	  no customer that ever asked to tell the two apart.
