615	### D-017 — Framework-owned simulation loop with a stepper seam
616	
617	**Status.** ratified
618	
619	**Position.** Framework-owned simulation loop; stepper seam (advance by
620	arbitrary `h` + on-demand dense output over the last step; one-step methods
621	only); in-house fixed-step RK4/Heun as the sole first-cut backends;
622	`OrdinaryDiffEq` dropped from dependency to possible future extension adapter.
623	
624	**Spec.** [§10.1][s10-1], [§10.2][s10-2]
625	
626	**Rationale.** Recorded only through the rejections below.
627	
628	**Rejected.**
629	- *`OrdinaryDiffEq` as substrate with `CallbackSet` choreography:* semantics by
630	  convention in a foreign event loop; demonstrated churn — the
631	  `task_local_storage` regression — in exactly the interactive multi-task
632	  usage. The substrate rejection's evidence dossier, from FlightCore's own
633	  `sim.jl`: a hand-rolled periodic callback with explicit `add_tstop!`
634	  bookkeeping after a DiffEqCallbacks release moved `PeriodicCallback` onto
635	  `task_local_storage`, per-evaluation state copying in and out of the
636	  integrator, stateless models integrating a dummy `[0.0]`, and log saving
637	  detoured through `deepcopy` in a `SavingCallback` — each a tax paid to
638	  express our semantics in someone else's loop, which `run!` was already
639	  driving manually anyway.
640	- *Fused loop without the seam:* loses the adaptive/stiff escape hatch for
641	  ~zero savings.
642	- *Multistep methods:* history rebuild after every handler.
