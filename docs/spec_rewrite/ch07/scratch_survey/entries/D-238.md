8873	### D-238 — Decide embed-accept on the type: lift the arrival, compare exactly
8874	
8875	**Status.** ratified
8876	
8877	**Position.** A type `V` is accepted at a declaration `P` at activation `T`
8878	when lifting `V`'s `Float64` positions to `T` exactly where `P` has `T`
8879	yields `P` itself, compared by identity. [D-235][d-235]'s always-on check and the
8880	concrete arm of [D-236][d-236]'s wire relation both take this form.
8881	
8882	**Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§9.5][s9-5]
8883	
8884	**Rationale.** [D-166][d-166] states embed-accept per leaf: a declared-`T` leaf
8885	accepts the scalar or a `Float64`, every other leaf is exact. The built
8886	relation checked that statement on the leaves — a struct's name and field
8887	types, a static array's size and eltype — and was exact on the leaves while
8888	blind to the type around them: a `NamedTuple`'s field names, a non-numeric
8889	type parameter and an array's mutability are type parameters, not leaves, so
8890	`(b = T,)` passed at a `(a = T,)` entry, `Tagged{:b}` at `Tagged{:a}` and an
8891	`MVector` at an `SVector`, each surfacing later as a raw `FieldError` or
8892	running silently (increment 32's review). A parallel walk over the two types'
8893	parameters, lifting a `Float64` in `V` where `P` has `T` and leaving every
8894	other position as written, is [D-166][d-166]'s statement decided on the type: [§9.5][s9-5]'s
8895	exact match at nominal is the case where nothing lifts and the relation is
8896	`===`, and the embedding is the one asymmetry. The lift is positional on the
8897	type, so it also reaches a `T` that carries no scalar, such as a tag parameter
8898	(`Tag{T}` with no field of type `T`): the store holds leaves and rebuilds the
8899	declaration from them, so `Tag{Float64}` arriving at `Tag{D8}` is normalized
8900	to the declaration rather than converted, the pinned-into-walking direction
8901	[D-236][d-236] already admits, while `Tag{D8}` at `Tag{Float64}` stays refused. A
8902	parameterless type compares by identity, so [D-237][d-237]'s opaque leaves need no arm
8903	of their own.
8904	
8905	**Rejected.**
8906	- *Superseded position — the leafwise walk:* exact on leaves, blind to the
8907	  parameters that distinguish two concrete types with the same leaves.
8908	- *`<:` with embedding on concrete declarations:* concrete types are final, so
8909	  `<:` is identity there and adds nothing; the abstract case is [D-236][d-236]'s.
8910	- *Acceptance by `convert`:* admits every lossy or renaming conversion Julia
8911	  defines, which is the convert-on-write [D-053][d-053] rejects.
