6945	### D-194 — Retire the `w` channel: intermediates are declared ports
6946	
6947	**Status.** ratified
6948	
6949	**Position.** The private `w` channel is deleted on both tiers. Every output
6950	stage returns its port NamedTuple alone, and a cross-stage intermediate is an
6951	ordinary declared port, reaching its consumers through the existing views.
6952	
6953	- The `(y, w)` return form, the one-hop law, the explicit re-return idiom and
6954	  the `w` bundle field are retired; stage returns are `y`, period.
6955	- An intermediate a later function needs is declared in `output_types` and
6956	  read where the bundle law already delivers it: stage-1 products through
6957	  `y_x`, everything through `y` in `f`, `g`, guards and handlers.
6958	- Round fusion ceases to be a design constraint on the executor ([§9.7][s9-7]):
6959	  passes communicate only through the table and the stores, no value crosses
6960	  a phase-body or chunk seam, and the phase-body arities carry the tick index
6961	  alone.
6962	- `w`'s nominal-only conformance carve-out is retired with it: promoted
6963	  intermediates carry declared types ([D-166][d-166]), embed-accept keeps the
6964	  constant-branch idiom legal, and the [§9.5][s9-5] check covers them at every
6965	  activation.
6966	- `DeadStage`'s ground simplifies: a stage returning `(;)` produces no ports
6967	  and is dead ([§9.3][s9-3]).
6968	- Visibility stays binary and [D-034][d-034]/[D-055][d-055] stay closed: no `Private` level
6969	  is added, the enclosing assembly's faces remain the scoping surface ([§8.6][s8-6]),
6970	  the [§4.3][s4-3] granularity guideline (one struct-valued bundle port) remains the
6971	  mitigation, and [D-034][d-034]'s `Private(T)` fallback-on-record remains the door.
6972	- The workspace and the handler return law are untouched: `ws` is the
6973	  mutable-scratch channel ([§7.3][s7-3]), handlers return stores ([D-090][d-090]).
6974	
6975	**Spec.** [§4.2][s4-2], [§4.3][s4-3], [§5.2][s5-2], [§5.3][s5-3], [§5.4][s5-4], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.3][s8-3], [§9.1][s9-1], [§9.3][s9-3], [§9.5][s9-5], [§9.7][s9-7],
6976	[Appendix B][sB], [Appendix C][sC], [Appendix D][sD]
6977	
6978	**Rationale.** `w` served two needs, and each is served better without it.
6979	Recomputation avoidance is the table's existing job: evaluating the RHS means
6980	running the sweep ([§5.3][s5-3]), so `f` always reads cells its own sweep just wrote,
6981	and a stage-1 product reaches stage 2 as `y_x` — freshness by the same
6982	ordering that makes the table sound everywhere. Privacy had no demonstrated
6983	customer: the FlightCore lineage record is that intermediates were only ever
6984	inspected by inclusion in the model output, which [D-165][d-165] itself recorded as the
6985	promote-to-output inspection path.
6986	
6987	What [D-165][d-165] could not see, and the kernel prototype's increment-3 design work
6988	surfaced, is where the SSA hand-off lands: the one-hop pairs span phase
6989	bodies, so `w` must thread through the [§9.7][s9-7] roster's call signatures and every
6990	chunk seam between producer and consumer, round fusion hardens from
6991	optimization into obligation, and under [§10.5][s10-5]'s gating a not-due producer
6992	forces `Union`-shaped slots into the boundary walk's collections. That is
6993	framework-special handling concentrated in the executor's hottest structure,
6994	purchased for a conformance check that runs at the nominal activation only —
6995	probe-observed types having no anchor off-nominal. A declared port rides
6996	machinery that already exists, is already measured, and is checked at every
6997	activation.
6998	
6999	Simulink is corroborating precedent: S-Functions share expensive intermediates
7000	through engine-ordered work vectors (`DWork` written in `mdlOutputs`, read in
7001	`mdlDerivatives`, fresh at every minor step by call order — the same
7002	sweep-precedes-`f` argument), and block diagrams publish every intermediate as
7003	a subsystem-scoped signal. No private inter-method channel exists there.
7004	
7005	**Rejected.**
7006	- *Keep `w` (the [D-165][d-165] position):* the threading cost above; a whole second
7007	  dataflow vocabulary (return law, hop law, bundle-presence rule,
7008	  probe-observed conformance regime) for values the table can carry.
7009	- *Auxiliary stateless leaf as the mechanism (assembly refactor):* forced
7010	  decomposition taxes the common case, and with `y` withheld from `f` a
7011	  component's own state-derived intermediate would round-trip through
7012	  external wiring to reach its own `f`. Remains available as an ordinary
7013	  modeling choice.
7014	- *Cells with probe-observed types (declaration-free):* [D-165][d-165]'s type-sourcing
7015	  objection stands unweakened — the probe observes one branch, so the cell
7016	  type detonates branch-dependently at non-nominal activations.
7017	- *`Private{P}`-tagged output entries:* re-litigated here and kept closed;
7018	  [D-034][d-034]'s no-demonstrated-customer ground and the central binary
7019	  visibility rule survive, [D-055][d-055]'s "first wrapper type" ground has expired
7020	  ([D-185][d-185]'s `Relative`/`Absolute`), and the fallback stays on record.
