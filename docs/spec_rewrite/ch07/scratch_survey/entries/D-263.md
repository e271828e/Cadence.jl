10256	### D-263 — One arity on both tiers: plain contracts, the `Pinned` marker and the mandatory store
10257	
10258	**Status.** ratified
10259	
10260	**Position.** Every contract declaration takes the component alone on both
10261	tiers, is written at nominal `Float64`, and is retyped by the framework. The
10262	tier is never spelled in a signature.
10263	
10264	- On the continuous tier `input_types` and `output_types` are **walked**: the
10265	  leaf walk that types `init_x` and the root inputs is applied to the declared
10266	  types, so every `Float64` position follows the activation scalar. A leaf
10267	  wrapped as `Pinned{P}` is pinned at every activation, and the wrapper is
10268	  stripped at nominal. On the discrete tier the declarations pin wholesale,
10269	  as before, and a `Pinned` entry there is `DeclarationOnWrongTier`.
10270	- The permissive reading of `input_types` ([D-167][d-167]) stands with the marker as
10271	  its spelling: an unpinned position is tolerant, a `Pinned` one demands a
10272	  frozen arrival. Both wire clauses of [§6.1][s6-1] stand, decided at the marker
10273	  scalar by retyping the declarations instead of calling them.
10274	- The type walk substitutes `Float64` in type-parameter positions, yields `P`
10275	  at `Pinned{P}`, and does not enter a mutable type's parameters. A handle's
10276	  data references are fields and never walk; a handle with a scalar parameter
10277	  walks like any type, and `Pinned` freezes one built from build-time data.
10278	- `init_workspace(::C, ::Type{T})` keeps its scalar and takes it on **both**
10279	  tiers; the framework passes `Float64` to a discrete allocator at every
10280	  activation.
10281	- Every leaf declares exactly one of `init_x` and `init_s`, and a stateless
10282	  leaf declares it empty, `init_x(::C) = (;)` or `init_s(::C) = (;)`. The
10283	  store is the tier marker, mandatory even when empty, as `child_connections`
10284	  is the class marker ([§8.5][s8-5]). `StoreWithoutUpdate` applies to a non-empty
10285	  store only.
10286	- `TierSignatureMismatch` retires with all three arms. `TierUnreadable` names
10287	  a primitive declaring neither store, and an empty-store leaf with no
10288	  `output_types` is `StatelessWithoutOutputs`.
10289	
10290	**Spec.** [§4.3][s4-3], [§6.1][s6-1], [§6.2][s6-2], [§7.2][s7-2], [§7.3][s7-3], [§8.1][s8-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§8.3][s8-3], [§8.5][s8-5], [§8.6][s8-6], [§9.1][s9-1],
10291	[§9.4][s9-4], [§9.5][s9-5], [§13.7][s13-7], [§14.10][s14-10], [Appendix A][sA], [Appendix C][sC], [Appendix D][sD]
10292	
10293	**Rationale.** The two-argument form charged every continuous leaf for a
10294	choice almost none of them make: the suite carries 11 pinned leaves against
10295	192 `T`-form declarations, and a flight library's components promote by
10296	default because trim and linearization sweep whole assemblies. [D-166][d-166]'s own
10297	criterion says a `T` belongs in a signature exactly where the declaration's
10298	non-nominal behavior is underdetermined by its nominal restriction. With a
10299	leaf-level marker that underdetermination is gone: the plain declaration,
10300	`Pinned` included, fixes the declaration at every scalar, so the signature's
10301	`T` would record nothing the page does not already say. The retype walk is
10302	not new machinery. It is `retype`, the walk `init_x`, conditions and the
10303	root-input cells already go through; the contracts were the one declaration
10304	kind it did not reach, and carrying two conventions cost more than carrying
10305	one.
10306	
10307	[D-166][d-166] reversed [D-079][d-079] on two grounds. Deliberate pinning of a real leaf was
10308	inexpressible under the plain form, and the plain form was reader-opaque,
10309	misleading to anyone not carrying the walk rule in their head. The marker is
10310	the element neither entry had on the table, and it answers the first ground
10311	outright: the pin is on the page, per leaf, schema-visible and
10312	conformance-checked, so [§14.10][s14-10]'s freeze door and the FFI door survive as
10313	declared doors. The second ground is revalued rather than answered. The walk
10314	rule is one rule, already taught for the state side; a reader who knows that
10315	`Float64` follows the scalar on the continuous tier reads every declaration
10316	in the framework with it.
10317	
10318	The forgotten-`T` classes vanish rather than move. A `Float64` written at a
10319	participating leaf now walks, which is what the author meant. The class that
10320	takes its place, a forgotten pin on an FFI leaf, fails where the forgotten
10321	`T` did and never silently: an input pin is a wire check at the first
10322	nominal build only when written, and an output built from frozen data is
10323	refused at the first `Dual` activation by the identity comparison on the
10324	opaque leaf or by the `MethodError` inside the user's math, because no lossy
10325	`Dual → Float64` cast exists. The embedding guarantee ([§9.5][s9-5]) is untouched.
10326	
10327	A stateless leaf loses its only tier signal with the arity, and the store
10328	supplies it. [§3.1][s3-1]'s continuous component has a state vector that may be
10329	empty, so a stateless continuous leaf is honestly spelled as a continuous
10330	leaf with zero state fields, `init_x(::C) = (;)`, and a sampled map as
10331	`init_s(::C) = (;)`. The empty NamedTuple is already the vocabulary's "no
10332	state": the bundle law puts a store's letter in the bundle only when the
10333	store is non-empty, so nothing downstream changes. Making the declaration
10334	mandatory is the move [§8.5][s8-5] already makes for `child_connections`,
10335	mandatory even when empty because defining it is the class marker; the store
10336	is the tier marker on the same terms. Every leaf's tier is then on the page
10337	in one place, stateful or not, with no tier by omission. The alternative
10338	that was on the table, an `is_discrete(::C)` trait defaulting to `false`,
10339	would have added a family name and a default, and it would have widened
10340	[D-178][d-178]'s recorded gap: an optional declaration lost to a local scope or a
10341	forgotten import silently drops its feature, so a sampler whose
10342	`is_discrete = true` was lost would silently become a transparent
10343	continuous wire, where a lost `init_s(::C) = (;)` fails loud as a leaf
10344	declaring no store. [D-173][d-173]'s and [D-178][d-178]'s rejections of the trait therefore
10345	stand. The cost is one line on every stateless leaf, the same line an
10346	assembly author already writes for `child_connections`.
10347	
10348	The workspace keeps its scalar because its declaration is by allocation, not
10349	by type. Retyping a type is a pure substitution and total over the
10350	vocabulary; retyping an allocated value is undefined for a factorization, a
10351	plan or a buffer whose size depends on the scalar, which is [D-077][d-077]'s standing
10352	rejection of `similar`-based re-scalaring. The scalar is consumed by the
10353	allocation, so it is not ceremony in the contract sense. Taking it on both
10354	tiers removes the last arity-shaped tier fact: the discrete tier never runs
10355	off-nominal, so `Float64` is the only scalar its allocator can honestly
10356	receive; the ceremony falls on a rare declaration; and [§14.10][s14-10]'s sampled-data
10357	door would bring the scalar there anyway.
10358	
10359	Opaque leaves need no stop of their own. The type walk substitutes
10360	parameters and never looks at fields, so a bulk-data handle whose references
10361	are fields passes through untouched, which is [§4.4][s4-4]'s frozen build-time data
10362	by construction. A handle with a scalar parameter is the case [D-237][d-237] admits, a
10363	different cell type per activation built at `T` by the stage; it walks like
10364	any type, and `Pinned` spells [D-237][d-237]'s other remedy. A mutable type's
10365	parameters pin by rule because no stage can produce a `Vector{Dual}` inside a
10366	handle without copying the grid at every evaluation, so no choice exists
10367	there for a marker to record.
10368	
10369	Annotation (2026-09-25): the marker is read at the top of an entry alone, and
10370	one below it is `IllegalPortType` on both tiers ([D-265][d-265]); a frozen opaque leaf
10371	is admitted at a tolerant entry ([D-264][d-264]); the marker's meaning is "carries no
10372	partials", and an AD-opaque implementation that must participate supplies a
10373	local rule under a tolerant entry ([D-266][d-266]).
10374	
10375	**Rejected.**
10376	- *Superseded position — the mandated two-argument forms on the continuous
10377	  tier ([D-166][d-166], [D-167][d-167]):* ceremony on every continuous leaf for a per-leaf
10378	  choice made on few; reader honesty bought at the price of a second typing
10379	  convention beside the state side's walk; the whole-signature and per-leaf
10380	  forgotten-`T` classes it had to contain exist only because the form exists.
10381	- *A one-argument `init_workspace` with framework re-scalaring:* [D-077][d-077]'s
10382	  grounds stand; reconstruction cannot cover plans, factorizations or
10383	  scalar-dependent structure.
10384	- *Keeping `init_workspace` tier-split:* one arity would stay a tier fact,
10385	  with its vote, its `DeclarationOnWrongTier` arm and a paragraph explaining
10386	  why this declaration alone still splits.
10387	- *A scalar-only marker (`PinnedFloat64`):* cannot pin an `SVector{3,Float64}`
10388	  or a handle whole; the parametric marker pins any leaf type.
10389	- *A parameter-position pin (`MyStruct{Float64, Pinned{Float64}}`):* fails a
10390	  `<: Real` bound unless `Pinned <: Real`, a lie in the type lattice; the
10391	  leaf-level pin covers every known case, and the limit is recorded.
10392	- *Stopping the type walk at every opaque leaf:* removes the handle-with-a-
10393	  scalar-parameter case [D-237][d-237] admits, and re-imports the inexpressibility
10394	  [D-166][d-166] charged [D-079][d-079] with.
10395	- *An `is_discrete(::C)` trait, default `false`:* a new family name and a
10396	  tier by omission; an optional declaration a local scope or a forgotten
10397	  import drops silently, turning a sampler into a transparent wire.
10398	- *Reading a stateless leaf's tier from its parent's `sample_times`:* a
10399	  block that is discrete by design, a `ZOH` or a sampler, could not say so,
10400	  and a user who forgets the entry gets a continuous wire with no ground for
10401	  the build to complain; substituting a stateless map into an unlisted slot
10402	  would silently flip its tier; the tier is the leaf's own fact ([D-056][d-056]).
10403	- *`nothing` as the empty store:* a sentinel return, the class [D-178][d-178] refuses
10404	  and `StoreNotNamedTuple` ([D-247][d-247]) already rejects; the empty NamedTuple
10405	  says the same thing inside the vocabulary.
10406	- *A stateless leaf with no outputs as a legal no-op:* admits a component
10407	  that produces nothing and stores nothing.
10408	- *Accepting `Pinned` silently on a discrete leaf:* a spelling that says
10409	  nothing is the class the declaration layer refuses.
