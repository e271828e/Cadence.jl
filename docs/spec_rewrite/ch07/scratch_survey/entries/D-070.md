2022	### D-070 — Trim service: in-house dense LM behind a swappable backend
2023	
2024	**Status.** ratified
2025	
2026	**Position.** The trim service defaults to an in-house dense LM solver behind a
2027	value-passed backend contract: the `NLoptBackend` extension takes squared
2028	residuals, putting today's algorithm one keyword away, and core carries zero
2029	optimizer deps.
2030	
2031	- Box bounds are applied by step projection, with saturated-at-solution flagged
2032	  in the report.
2033	- Scratch store sets are instantiated per invocation from activation layouts —
2034	  the layout reusable, the buffers dying with the call, Dual un-aliasability
2035	  being defense in depth and not the mechanism — while authoritative stores have
2036	  exactly one writer, the commit through boundary zero.
2037	- `TrimReport` is a no-throw structured value: non-convergence is an expected
2038	  envelope-sweep outcome, a malformed problem a `BuildError` at setup.
2039	- The AD obligation is scoped to continuous stages, `f` and user
2040	  assignment/residual math, the discrete tier frozen-exact — identical to
2041	  linearization's activation, and build-checked by the Dual probe.
2042	- The C172 audit is Interpolations tables (prefer cubic knots), saturation
2043	  rank-deficiency (LM-tolerated, reported), gear zero airborne.
2044	
2045	**Spec.** [§14.8][s14-8]
2046	
2047	**Rationale.** Recorded only through the rejections below.
2048	
2049	**Rejected.**
2050	- *External NLS packages:* heavy dep for ~100 lines; [§10.2][s10-2] stepper precedent;
2051	  per-residual tolerance test not natively spelled.
2052	- *NLopt as core dependency:* no LM; fallback-only role.
2053	- *Iterating on the nominal activation's singleton buffers:* aliases the sim's
2054	  authoritative stores — warn-but-assign reborn.
2055	- *Throw-on-non-convergence:* an expected outcome, not broken machinery.
