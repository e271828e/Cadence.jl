3185	### D-107 — Match trace record density to batch density, not surface width
3186	
3187	**Status.** ratified
3188	
3189	**Position.** Trace records match batch density: enumerated writers' positional
3190	batches are retained verbatim, zero-copy (claim-narrow, dense by nature); the
3191	interactive writer's wide mostly-`nothing` tuple is converted at the drain on
3192	retention — (position ⇒ value) pairs for the non-`nothing` entries, an
3193	O(surface-width) scan and one small allocation at most once per frame, only on
3194	interactive-active frames, inside the [§7.5][s7-5] retention carve-out the log already
3195	occupies — so trace size tracks information, not surface width, preserving
3196	[D-029][d-029]'s two-orders-below-the-log budget at every world scale.
3197	
3198	**Spec.** [§7.5][s7-5], [§11.5][s11-5], [§12.7][s12-7]
3199	
3200	**Rationale.** The header carries each writer's face-name → position schema
3201	(positional records are meaningless without it and replay does not reconstruct
3202	claims); replay pays the inverse conversion once, up front, in the [§12.7][s12-7]
3203	validation pass — no name resolved per frame under replay either; conversion
3204	site is the drain, not the staging shim (the drained tuple is the coalesced
3205	truth; a shim-side sparse log would need its own merge).
3206	
3207	**Rejected.**
3208	- *Verbatim retention of interactive positional batches:* size tracks surface
3209	  width × edit rate — at hundreds of unclaimed faces, render-rate dragging
3210	  inflates the trace past the budget that justifies trace-on-by-default; a
3211	  mostly-`nothing` tuple is also schema-bound and hostile to inspection.
3212	- *Sparse records for enumerated writers too:* their batches are dense — sparse
3213	  encoding adds names for nothing.
3214	- *Shim-side sparse recording:* needs its own merge to match coalescing.
3215	- *Keeping name-keyed replay application:* re-imports the dynamic drain path
3216	  replay exists to avoid.
