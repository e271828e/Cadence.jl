10411	### D-264 — Admit a frozen opaque leaf at a tolerant entry
10412	
10413	**Status.** ratified
10414	
10415	**Position.** The wire relation admits a pinned or discrete producer of an
10416	opaque leaf at an unpinned entry of that leaf's type, as the producer's cell.
10417	The identity rule for an opaque leaf ([D-237][d-237]) governs a store and the
10418	producer's own conformance check alone, never a wire.
10419	
10420	**Spec.** [§6.1][s6-1], [§8.2][s8-2], [§9.5][s9-5], [Appendix C][sC]
10421	
10422	**Rationale.** The refusal came from one predicate serving two questions.
10423	`_accepts` decides both the store's conformance check and the wire relation,
10424	and at a store its opaque short-circuit is essential: a producer's output
10425	cell at a `Dual` activation is a `Vector{Handle{Dual}}` when the declaration
10426	walks, and a `Handle{Float64}` cannot be placed there without the framework
10427	constructing a handle, which it refuses to do. At a wire nothing is
10428	constructed. The entry is a bound, and the consumer gathers the producer's
10429	cell as stored. Embedding at a tolerant scalar entry is a type-level
10430	admission plus Julia's promotion inside the consumer's math, and a handle
10431	behaves identically: the consumer reads `u.terrain.h0::Float64` beside `Dual`
10432	state and its arithmetic promotes. The derivative through the handle is zero
10433	along every seeded direction, and that is the true derivative, because the
10434	producer declared that no partials enter it. [§9.5][s9-5]'s argument for why a
10435	`Float64` at a walking leaf is honest applies unchanged. [D-238][d-238]'s parameter
10436	walk already gives the right answer once the short-circuit is lifted: lifting
10437	`Handle{Float64}` at the parameter position where the entry has `T` yields
10438	`Handle{Dual}`, the entry itself.
10439	
10440	The positive ground is [§8.2][s8-2]'s substitution promise, that a walking producer,
10441	a frozen discrete producer and a root input are all admissible behind a
10442	tolerant entry. For handles that promise was broken in both frozen forms. A
10443	library's landing gear declaring `terrain = TerrainField{Float64}` could not
10444	be fed by a static terrain source at all, and its author could not repair it
10445	without pinning the entry, which would then refuse a moving deck instead.
10446	The wire pass reported the direction under `WalkingFaceAtFrozenEntry` with
10447	the endpoints reversed, calling the entry frozen and the producer walking.
10448	
10449	Two risks are specific to handles, and both fail loudly at the `Dual` probe.
10450	For numbers Julia promotes a `Float64` and a `Dual` in any expression; for a
10451	handle there is no such machinery, so a consumer signature that ties the
10452	handle's parameter to another argument's (`height(f::H{T}, p::SVector{3,T})
10453	where {T}`) fails with a `MethodError` whenever the handle is frozen and the
10454	position is not. That is the genericity obligation a tolerant entry already
10455	claims, checked by the probe as [§8.2][s8-2] says. And pass-through is refused where
10456	scalar pass-through is embedded: a consumer that re-emits a frozen handle
10457	under an unpinned output hits the store's identity rule, so a handle's pin is
10458	a property of its chain, and every component that forwards it pins that
10459	output too. No silent wrong Jacobian was found. The misplaced-pin class is
10460	covered identically: a pinned producer that builds `Handle(x.h + 1.0, z)` at
10461	a `Dual` activation produces `Handle{Dual}`, the identity check refuses it,
10462	and the pin hint fires. `companions/handle_walk_walkthrough.md` works the
10463	example through.
10464	
10465	**Rejected.**
10466	- *A named, collected refusal for the direction (a frozen face at a walking
10467	  entry), with remedies to pin the entry or build the handle at `T`:* breaks
10468	  the substitution promise for every frozen handle producer, discrete ones
10469	  included, and asks a library consumer to choose between pinning its entry
10470	  and refusing every static source; the refusal was the store's identity rule
10471	  applied to a question it was not written for.
10472	- *An embedding for opaque leaves (constructing the handle at the entry):*
10473	  the framework never builds handles; a cross-eltype construction can be a
10474	  grid copy per evaluation, which [D-237][d-237]'s identity rule exists to refuse.
